# Death grip: research notes

Gathered 2026-11-14 at James's request. "Death grip" is slang for the habit James described: years of hard, fast, often dry masturbation that leaves a man unable to finish with a woman (delayed ejaculation), or unable to stay hard with her. All quotes below are verbatim. Forum and YouTube commenters are anonymised.

## The short version for the writer

- **It's real enough that the clinicians treat it, but it isn't an official diagnosis.** "Death grip syndrome" is a slang term from a Dan Savage column in 2003. The clinical names are *idiosyncratic masturbatory style* (Perelman) and *traumatic masturbatory syndrome* (TMS, in urology papers).
- **The best evidence is one 2023 case-control study.** Young men with ED were 2.2 times more likely to have "atypical masturbatory behaviours" (hard pressure, prone rubbing), and were harder when masturbating than with a partner. That shows an association, not proof of cause, and the urologist who explains it says so.
- **The book's argument is the useful bit.** Even sex-positive doctors who say masturbation is fine tell these men to **stop masturbating first**. That fits James's line about the experts saying it's fine, without inventing anything. The experts don't claim it destroys a man. They simply prescribe stopping.
- **Recovery is real but slow, so be honest about that.** The stories run from about a month (a Brazilian student's first orgasm in sex after just over a month off) to six to eight months, and one man felt nothing at nine months. The book should promise freedom, not a timetable.
- **The "gentle masturbation" retraining fix is a trap in EasyPeasy terms.** It's cutting down by another name (Ch 11), and the forum itself argues about it. One woman whose ex had it put it plainly: it was "MO or a healthy relationship".

## Experts

**Michael A. Perelman, PhD** (sex therapist, Cornell; the leading writer on delayed ejaculation)
- "the primary factor causing DE is usually an 'idiosyncratic masturbatory style'" ... "a technique not easily duplicated by the partner during sex" ... "patterns of self-stimulation notable for one or more of the following idiosyncrasies: speed, pressure, duration" ... "High frequency (varies from man to man) masturbation is often associated with DE."
  [Psychology Today, "Why Delayed Ejaculation Is More Common Than Folks Realize", 31 Dec 2018](https://www.psychologytoday.com/gb/blog/sexual-tipping-point/201812/why-delayed-ejaculation-is-more-common-than-folks-realize)
- He also names a second cause: "there is a disparity between the reality of sex with their partners and the sexual fantasies". That ties into Ch 14 (the thought life).
- On treatment: "the man must temporarily suspend masturbatory activity". If a man refuses, the minimum is "no ejaculation within 72 hours" before sex with his partner. "It is often necessary to remind repeatedly that the need for such restraint is only temporary."
  [Psychology Today, "Delayed Ejaculation: Informed Diagnosis and Treatment", 31 Dec 2018](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/sexual-tipping-point/201812/delayed-ejaculation-informed-diagnosis-and-treatment)
- From his journal paper: "Discontinuing, reducing or altering masturbation is often required, which evokes patient resistance."
  [Perelman, *Translational Andrology and Urology* 5(4), 2016](https://tau.amegroups.org/article/view/11164)

**Rena Malik, MD** (urologist, YouTube; openly pro-masturbation)
- "your hand can create a lot more force and pressure than potentially a vagina can ... your body has now habituated to that sensation".
- On the effect in marriage: "it can be devastating because you're now having difficulty getting erections with someone who presumably you really like ... and then you're embarrassed to talk to your partner about masturbation".
- On what to do: "the first thing you have to do is stop masturbating now how long you have to do that for really depends on you how long have you been masturbating the same way how often do you do it".

- Her caveat, which the book should keep fair: "this is not to shame masturbation I don't think there is a problem with masturbation".
  [Everything You Need To Know About Death Grip, Explained by a Urologist (SexciPjCC0s)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SexciPjCC0s)

**The 2023 study** (Japanese Urological Association journal, PubMed 37605604)
- 448 men, average age 30. "Patients had 2.2-fold ... increased risks of having at least one AMB [atypical masturbatory behaviour], compared with controls." Conclusion: "Atypical masturbatory behaviors are more common in young men presenting with erectile dysfunction. These patients have higher erection hardness scores during masturbation compared to partnered sex."
  [Traumatic masturbation and erectile dysfunction: a matched case-control study](https://www.pubmed.gov/37605604/)

**Sexual Medicine Society of North America**
- "Conditioning by use of unique ("idiosyncratic") masturbation practices may cause men to experience delayed orgasm." Its example is a man "used to masturbating with a very fast motion".
  [SMSNA, Delayed Ejaculation](https://smsna.org/patients/conditions/delayed-ejaculation)

**Columbia's Go Ask Alice**
- "frequent masturbation that's combined with heavy pornography use is associated with delayed ejaculation".
  [Go Ask Alice, last edited 25 Mar 2026](https://goaskalice.columbia.edu/answered-questions/can-frequent-masturbation-affect-sexual-function)

**Sceptics** (use them for honesty)
- Urologist Richard Santucci said it "may be true for the individual person" but "I just don't believe it's that common", and he looks first at "diabetes, medications, low testosterone, anxiety". Dan Savage, who coined the term, admits: "It's anecdote, personal experience, and folk wisdom."
  [VICE, Mike Pearl, 28 Aug 2015](https://www.vice.com/en/article/is-death-grip-syndrome-a-masturbation-myth-293/)
- Wikipedia: the concept "is not recognized by any mainstream medical bodies."
  [Wikipedia: Death-grip syndrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death-grip_syndrome)
- **Writer's note:** the book should add one line telling a man with this problem to see a doctor about other causes (medications such as SSRIs, diabetes, low testosterone). Every expert source says this.

## Where it fits in the book

- **Ch 8, What It's Doing to You (main home).** This is the most concrete physical cost the book can name, and it has clinical backing, so it isn't only conviction. Use the Brazilian student, "She started to think it was her fault", the "dress rehearsal" line, and Dr Malik's "devastating". Present the 2023 study as an association. Add a line telling men to see a doctor about other causes.
- **Ch 4, The World's Lies.** Even a urologist who says "I don't think there is a problem with masturbation" says "the first thing you have to do is stop masturbating". Perelman says the same. That gives James's "the experts say it's fine" point an honest form.
- **Ch 13, Types of User, "I'll stop when I'm married".** The habit follows you into the marriage bed. Use the older remarried man and "the girl I love's virginity".
- **Ch 11, Beware of Cutting Down, and Ch 24, Substitutes.** "Gentle, lubricated retraining" and sleeves are cutting down and substitutes. The forum woman's "MO or a healthy relationship" and "if he moed no matter how light he would struggle" answer them.
- **Ch 14, Fantasy.** Perelman's fantasy-versus-reality "disparity", and Noah's "don't start ... fantasizing about porn ... to get yourself there".
- **Ch 21, The First Weeks.** The flatline ("my penis literally 'died' on me"), and the fact that it passed.
- **Ch 28, Feedback ("How long until things work again?").** Be honest that it takes from about a month to many months, and it varies. Perelman: "only temporary". Give the Noah timeline. Don't promise a date.
- **Ch 30, Advice to Non-users (wives and partners).** "She started to think it was her fault." The resentful partner who "was not told about it until late". Tell wives it isn't about her attractiveness, and tell husbands to tell her. One commenter asked for exactly this ("how the wife can help the husband").

## Cautions

- Don't say doctors have proved masturbation causes this. Say "doctors see it", "the habit trains the body", and "one study found men with ED were twice as likely to masturbate this way".
- Keep it brief and clean, in the book's register. No technique talk and no "light as a feather" instructions.
- Rare but real: some men don't recover quickly (the nine-month comment). Grace, not a guarantee.
