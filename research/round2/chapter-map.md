## Chapter by chapter: the strongest new material

Only the best one to three items per chapter. More are in the log.

- **Intro.** A commenter: "It's been over 5 years since God broke me free from the chains of pornography and masturbation addiction. I will never go back." Ezekiel's story is the sequel's premise in one life: "Before I ever watched porn ... I was engaging in that act." A talkingsober user who read EasyPeasy blocked porn "successfully, but I had/have problem with MO".
- **2. Why is it difficult?** How it starts: a fourth-grade lunch table, then the church restroom at 10 (Ezekiel); a pop-up ad at 6 (Jotivation). "I've been struggling with lust for 14 years. Worst part? I'm only 23."
- **3. The Flesh.** "Poisonous honey" (Captain Sinbad's image for the bait). "You think that you're relieving yourself ... but you're making the stronghold more reinforced ... adding the brick and mortar for your own prison" (Ezekiel). Challies via Groeschel: "Temptation is anything that promises satisfaction at the cost of your obedience to God."
- **4. The World's Lies.** "It's natural": "We also see in nature where pigs eat trash" (Ezekiel). The pastor's fair admission that the Bible never names masturbation (Novotny), and the church's silence: "This is a subject that is not addressed in the pulpit at all!" (commenter).
- **5. The Times We Reach For It.** "It's not that I'm horny I'm just depressed sad and I need dopamine ... I don't know how to handle being sad" (commenter, 5K likes). Ezekiel: "you might not have even felt lustful at the time ... It might be loneliness." Groeschel on tired, angry, lonely and hurting; "I'm bored, what should I do with my time?" as step one.
- **6. What Am I Giving Up?** EasyPeasy reader: "I was placing value on my DOC and felt that I was making a sacrifice by choosing to quit."
- **7. Time.** "I can't tell you how many years I've wasted battling with the lust" (Nick Jones). Captain Sinbad's "strategic move": pour the time into one goal you can reach in three or four months.
- **8. What It's Doing to You (the drive chapter).** Three independent voices support James's conviction:
  - Pastor Novotny (married): the drive is there to make him pursue his wife, "but if I take that drive ... mindlessly masturbating ... I don't have it anymore so let's just let her do her thing ... I'm gonna read a book".
  - Nick Jones: "the purpose of lust is to make you comfortable ... satisfied ... content with where you are in your life."
  - Captain Sinbad: "so many of us since we were like 12 years old had been killing that desire you know through pornography through masturbation."
  - Plus: "masturbation drained me to the core" and "it clouds the mind and acts like a slow poison" (commenters), and Fradd: "I know what it's like to feel my desire for God fade as my craving for pleasure grew."
- **10. Willpower.** The comment-section ritual: "Day 1 of quitting ... Like this comment so I always get reminders! ... once and for all" (posted again and again). Ezekiel: "I was obsessing over my sin ... the foundation of my repentance was me." Washer: "No, you're not strong enough, so don't do it." "Every time I think I've escaped this sin, it comes back at me like a bite of a snake."
- **11. Cutting Down.** "God told me I couldn't kill something I continue to feed" (commenter). *Every Man's Battle*: "Excellence is a mixed standard, while obedience is a fixed standard."
- **12 and 22. Just Once / One Peek.** Satan's voice: "it's just one more time it's just one more video ... you can get back to your purpose after" (Nick Jones). The diet image: after a long break, "the moment you taste it, it's like it opens you up into a world of junk food" (Ezekiel).
- **13. Types of User.** *No-fantasy user:* "I didn't need sexual thoughts to engage in it" (Ezekiel); "what if I'm just staring at a tree out the window?" "Well, that's concerning" (Novotny). *"I'll stop when I'm married":* "Marriage does not solve the problem" (r/TrueChristian); "When I was married, I brought that into my marriage" (commenter); the 1996 pastor: "Marriage didn't help." *Married man:* Weiss on "intimacy anorexia".
- **14. Thought Life.** Groeschel's four steps (thought, imagination, justification, choice) and his own two-second hesitation on a bikini channel: his wife asked, "Well, was it worth it?" Contrast this with Captain Sinbad's advice to train "at a gym with attractive women" and "convert ... lust into ... inspiration". The book's answer is Matthew 5:28.
- **15. A Secret Habit.** Weiss: "Masturbation is a secret and as long as it stays a secret, you will probably not get better." His own turn came when he told his roommate. The 1996 pastor: "I needed a confessor." Groeschel: "You're only as strong as you are honest."
- **19. False Incentives.** Captain Sinbad's "Women Can Smell the Retention on You", law of attraction and Napoleon Hill's "sex transmutation", set against his own caveats ("I hate the fact that they can't be backed up more scientifically"; it won't "create desire from women who aren't already interested"). Weiss's rewards ("a vacation in Greece") and punishments ("eat a raw onion") show the willpower method's incentives.
- **20. The Easy Way.** "Our motive for repentance is the fact that God is good. Not the fact that you are bad" (Ezekiel). Groeschel on godly versus worldly sorrow. "'Breaking God's heart' just changed my whole entire mindset" (commenter).
- **21. The First Weeks.** QuitByHealing's "do nothing" method is EasyPeasy's urge advice in a coach's words: notice the urge, don't fight it, don't feed it, and it "fades after maybe a minute or two". Groeschel: "it's not a sin to be tempted." The flatline, reported by one man as days 40 to 100 ("the other side is beautiful"). A commenter: "I can be taking a nap in my bed then the feeling comes."
- **23. Harder for me?** "From 13 to 44, over 30 years of struggles. I am 48 now" (and free). "I'm fourteen and I need this."
- **25. Avoid Temptation?** Groeschel: "I have never eaten 400 M and Ms when I had no M and Ms within my reach" and "if every time you go to the gym you find yourself going 'Praise the Lord for that' ... don't go to the gym!" Ezekiel: "If it means you got to get a roommate, get a roommate."
- **26. Revelation.** Both kinds are on record: instant ("He purged me of those sins in one day", Desiring God) and gradual ("this wasn't something that happened overnight", Jotivation). Captain Sinbad's first ten days: "like Dorothy stepping out of Kansas".
- **28. Feedback / relapse.** "You are not your past you are how you respond to your past." "You need to stop 'keeping scores' as a way to gauge how delivered you are" (commenter). Ezekiel's prodigal: "But God, I still smell like yesterday. Just come."
- **30. Non-users.** Parents: "be the first presenter of truth to your children" (Ezekiel), and boys of 14 searching YouTube for help. Wives: Novotny's drive point and Weiss on starving a wife sexually. Women: several women commenters struggle with this and found "no one to talk to".

## Gaps in the current draft

1. **Abuse and trauma.** Many commenters, and Dr. Weiss ("80% [of my clients] are sexual abuse survivors"), tie compulsive masturbation to childhood sexual abuse. One commenter mentioned wanting to end his life. The draft has a crisis line, but no note on abuse. **Suggest:** a short, gentle paragraph in Ch 28 and the Appendix saying that if this started after abuse, telling someone and getting trauma-informed help is part of freedom, with pointers to such help (e.g. RAINN in the US, plus national equivalents).
2. **Masturbation without porn or fantasy.** This is the book's whole premise as a sequel, but the draft's testimonies are mostly about porn. Ezekiel's story and several commenters ("I don't need videos or even to think of anyone") fill this gap.
3. **The drive argument from a pastor.** Ch 8 rested on conviction and the FitXFearless material. Novotny gives it a mainstream pastoral voice, and Nick Jones gives it a young believer's voice.
4. **Emotional triggers.** Ch 5 can now quote men naming sadness, loneliness and boredom as the real trigger.
5. **Urges without fear.** Ch 21 has a ready-made, non-religious method that matches EasyPeasy (QuitByHealing), plus Groeschel's "not a sin to be tempted".
6. **Women's voices.** Women commenters struggle with this too and say no one talks about it. This supports the short women's notes in the Intro and Ch 30.
7. **Married men.** There is now material from Novotny, Weiss, the 1996 pastor and commenters.
8. **Reddit** is still unreached (see above).
9. **Revelation is gradual for many.** Ch 26 should allow for both instant and gradual freedom.

## Cautions

- Captain Sinbad's framing is Hindu brahmacharya, kundalini and law of attraction, and some of his advice feeds lust (stay near attractive women). Use him for story and honesty, never for method.
- Ezekiel's "spirits that feed on our pleasure" and some commenters' "spirit wife" ideas are charismatic beliefs. Leave them out.
- Weiss and QuitByHealing sell courses. Quote the idea, not the product.
- Comment likes and dates are as shown on 2026-11-14 ("1 year ago" and so on).
