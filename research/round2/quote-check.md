## DRAFT-NOTES flags this resolves
- **Ch 8, "destroyed four (4) marriages"**: the testimony continues "and left three children from two of those marriages", so they were his own marriages. Assumption confirmed.
- **Ch 30, Dobson**: the dobsonlibrary passage is about teenagers ("you will not stop the practice of masturbation in your teenagers"). Confirmed. Dobson's "not much of an issue with God" is from *Preparing for Adolescence*, quoted by Lauren Winner (Today's Christian Woman, Jan 2000).
- **Ch 4, NoFap's origin**: confirmed. Wikipedia says Rhodes started NoFap in June 2011 after a Reddit thread about a testosterone spike at seven days. **New:** the underlying study was retracted in 2021. That's a useful line for Ch 4 or 19.
- **Ch 6, Screwtape "diminishes pleasure while increasing desire"**: the full sentence is "In the first place it diminishes pleasure while increasing desire." It's about the demand for constant change (HarperOne 2001, pp. 135–137, i.e. Letter 25). Fair in context if the chapter applies it to novelty-seeking.
- **Lewis attributions**: "whimpering, whispering thing" is given as *The Great Divorce* on Catholic Education (secondary). The harem and prison lines are from the 1956 letter to Keith Masson. "Coming to love the prison" is on the Catholic Education page, but not on Pints with Jack. "Indulgence brings fog", "the least bad of all sins" and "sit down content with anything less than perfection" are from *Mere Christianity* III.5. "Necessary piece of apparatus" is from *The Four Loves*, ch. 5.
- **Jason Evert**: "Chastity is the virtue that frees you to love" is Evert's own. "What you really long for is authentic love, but you settled for the counterfeit" is Evert quoting an unnamed friend. Attribute accordingly.
- **"Pastor J.D."**: the page names only "Pastor J.D.", but it's on jdgreear.com, so J.D. Greear is safe. The full line continues "But we shouldn't indulge their sin either."
- **Talk Jesus**: the "sensational feeling of peace" followed a spiritual experience (receiving the Holy Ghost), not quitting itself. Frame it that way.
- **FitXFearless auto-caption quotes**: all 40 match the transcripts. Ch 5's "Touching myself is not rewarding." is in the transcript as captioned.
