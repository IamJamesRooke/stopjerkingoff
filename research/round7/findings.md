Checked 2026-11-19. Used in Chapter 4, "The lies about porn and about women".

## GirlsDoPorn
- US Attorney's Office, Southern District of California, press release, 8 Sept 2025,
  "GirlsDoPorn Owner Michael Pratt Sentenced to 27 Years for Sex Trafficking Hundreds of Women":
  https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdca/pr/girlsdoporn-owner-michael-pratt-sentenced-27-years-sex-trafficking-hundreds-women
  - Pleaded guilty 5 June 2025 to sex trafficking by force, fraud and coercion, and conspiracy.
  - Misleading modeling ads; women told the videos would never be posted online or
    released in the United States. Most victims were 18 to 21. "Hundreds of women."
  - Videos were sold on the company's own sites, and clips were posted on free sites
    including Pornhub, with view counts in the millions.
- Earlier sentences in the same case (same office): 20 years and 14 years for co-conspirators.

## Pornhub's owner (Aylo, formerly MindGeek)
- AP, 21 Dec 2023, "Pornhub owner agrees to pay $1.8M and independent monitor to resolve
  sex trafficking-related charge" (US Attorney, Eastern District of New York):
  https://www.wsls.com/business/2023/12/21/pornhub-owner-agrees-to-pay-18m-and-independent-monitor-to-resolve-sex-trafficking-related-charge/
  - Deferred prosecution agreement. Charge: unlawful monetary transactions involving
    sex trafficking proceeds (the GirlsDoPorn videos). Aylo "admitted to profiting from
    sex trafficking" but did not plead guilty. Over $1.8M to the government, payments
    to victims, a monitor for three years.
