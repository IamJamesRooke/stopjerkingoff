## How to read this file

- **Method.** Pages were read with WebFetch, which returns extracts through a summariser capped at about 125 characters per quote. So quotes below are short fragments. I asked for exact wording each time. **Spot-check against the page before printing**, especially any quote near a sentence end.
- **"UNVERIFIED wording"** means I did not see the exact words on a page. It is used for paraphrase and for anything I know from memory but did not see.
- **Blocked or failed:** curl and direct downloads are blocked by the egress proxy, so Gutenberg, CCEL and similar full texts could not be read whole. Where a public-domain text could be read through WebFetch I used it. The vatican.va audience page returned only navigation. The archive.org text of *Mere Christianity* cut off before Book III. bibletools.io failed.
- **Lewis is in copyright.** Every Lewis quote is a short fragment (all under 25 words). Do not extend them. Paraphrase the rest.
- **Not found:** nothing usable from Sam Allberry on masturbation (searches returned others' articles only). I have not invented anything for him.

## 1. C.S. Lewis

### 1.1 Letter to Keith Masson, 1956 (masturbation)

- **Source:** *The Collected Letters of C.S. Lewis, Volume 3* (letter to Keith Masson, 1956). Excerpts seen on:
  - https://www.pintswithjack.com/?p=341 (dated it 1956)
  - https://www.catholiceducation.org/en/marriage-and-family/sexuality/c-s-lewis-on-lust-women-and-masturbation.html (no date shown)
  - https://restlesspilgrim.net/blog/tag/sexual-morality/ (dated 1956)
- **Verbatim fragments:**
  - "sends the man back into the prison of himself, there to keep a harem of imaginary brides." (pintswithjack; catholiceducation)
  - "this harem, once admitted, works against his ever getting out and really uniting with a real woman." (catholiceducation)
  - "The danger is that of coming to love the prison." (catholiceducation; restlesspilgrim)
  - "Masturbation is to be avoided as all things are to be avoided which retard this process." (restlesspilgrim)
  - "almost the main work of life is to come out of our selves, out of the little dark prison we are all born in." (restlesspilgrim)
  - "they become merely the medium through which he increasingly adores himself." (restlesspilgrim)
- **Summary of the idea (paraphrase):** Sexual desire is meant to pull a man out of himself toward another person. The habit turns it back inward. The fantasy harem is always available, never says no and never asks for sacrifice. So a man who feeds it gets less able to love a real woman. The prison is comfortable, and the real danger is that he comes to love it. Lewis also frames it as a reason, not just a rule: the main work of life is getting out of the self.
- **Caveat for the book:** The pages quote only fragments. The whole letter, including Lewis's advice on guilt, was not read. UNVERIFIED wording for anything beyond the lines above.

## 2. Augustine, Confessions (public domain, E.B. Pusey translation)

All from https://faculty.georgetown.edu/jod/augustine/Pusey/book08 (Pusey, Book 8). Section numbers follow the page's numbering (chapter.section).

- **"Not yet" prayer, Book 8, ch. 7, §17:** "Give me chastity and continency, only not yet." (The Pusey wording is "continency", not "continence".)
- **Chain of habit, Book 8, ch. 5, §10:**
  - "not with another's irons, but by my own iron will"
  - "For of a forward will, was a lust made; and a lust served, became custom; and custom not resisted, became necessity." (Pusey says "custom", not "habit".)
  - "Thus did my two wills, one new, and the other old, one carnal, the other spiritual, struggle within me; and by their discord, undid my soul."
- **Old attachments, Book 8, ch. 11, §26:** "The very toys of toys, and vanities of vanities, my ancient mistresses, still held me." They whisper "Dost thou cast us off?"
- **Delay, Book 8, ch. 12, §28:** "how long? how long, Lord, wilt Thou be angry for ever?" and "to-morrow, and tomorrow?"
- **Tolle lege, Book 8, ch. 12, §29:** "Take up and read; Take up and read." The verse he read: "Not in rioting and drunkenness, not in chambering and wantonness, not in strife and envying;" then "make not provision for the flesh, in concupiscence." (The page does not label this as Romans 13. Reference is known from elsewhere: Romans 13:13-14; UNVERIFIED on this page.)
- **After:** "a light as it were of serenity infused into my heart" (ch. 12, §29).
- **Ideas for chapters:**
  - "Not yet" is the whole of Chapter 12 ("Just Once") and Chapter 10 ("Willpower"): he wanted to be freed and wanted not to be freed, both at once.
  - The chain: a lust fed becomes habit; habit unresisted becomes necessity. Perfect for "Why Is It Difficult to Stop" (02) and "Types of User" (13).
  - Two wills fighting is the Easyway "two voices" idea. The book can say: the war ends when one will is gone, not when one wins.
  - Tolle lege is the call for "The Final Step" (27): the turn came by hearing, not by grinding.
  - The old mistresses whispering "Dost thou cast us off?" is the "little monster" of the first days (21).

## 5. Practical facts (short)

### 5.1 Habit loops

- **Cue, craving, response, reward:** James Clear says "The process of building a habit can be divided into four simple steps: cue, craving, response, and reward." For breaking one he inverts the laws: "Make it invisible", "Make it unattractive", "Make it difficult", "Make it unsatisfying." https://jamesclear.com/atomic-habits-summary
- **Duhigg's version (cue, routine, reward):** only secondary summaries were returned in search (e.g. shortform.com, litcharts.com). I did not open them or the book. Cite *The Power of Habit* (2012) generally; **UNVERIFIED wording** for any quote.
- **Wendy Wood:**
  - "a stunning 43% of everyday actions are enacted habitually while people are thinking about something else" (Chris Palmer, *Monitor on Psychology*, 1 November 2020, https://apa.org/monitor/2020/11/career-lab-habits).
  - "habits can be broken by controlling the cues that trigger behavior." (same page)
  - Wood in Michaela Barnett's interview, *Behavioral Scientist*, 14 October 2019: "about 43 percent of what people do every day is repeated in the same context". She also says that to start a new habit "you have to exert self-control, and that's just not true" is a common belief. https://behavioralscientist.org/good-habits-bad-habits-a-conversation-with-wendy-wood/ (The second is her describing a belief she rejects. Do not present it as her advice.)
- **Use:** "Times We Reach For It" (05), "Why Is It Difficult" (02), "Instructions" (31).

### 5.2 Urge surfing (Alan Marlatt)

- Source: Marlatt GA, Chawla N. "Meditation and Alcohol Use." *Southern Medical Journal* 2007;100(4):451–453. https://depts.washington.edu/abrc/mbrp/reprints/MeditationAlcoholUse2007.pdf. The paper credits the technique to Marlatt (2002), *Cognitive and Behavioral Practice* 9:44–50 (not opened).
- Quotes: "visualize the urge or strong craving as an ocean wave"; the urge "begins as a small wavelet that gradually increases in magnitude until it builds up to a large cresting wave."; allow it "to first rise up and decline without being 'wiped out'".
- **Caution:** this is an alcohol paper. Say "developed for addictions", not "proven for masturbation".
- **Use:** "Temptation" (24), "First Weeks" (21). Pair with prayer: ride the wave with God, not alone.

### 5.3 Sperm production cycle: the 74-day claim is shaky

- Healthline (medically reviewed by Meredith Goodwin, MD, updated 27 October 2026): "The full process takes about 64 days." and "a full sperm regeneration cycle (spermatogenesis) takes a little over 2 months." https://www.healthline.com/health/mens-health/how-long-does-it-take-for-sperm-to-regenerate
- Harvard BioNumbers: "The duration of the whole spermatogenesis in man is about 64 days (primary sources)." Primary: Heller and Clermont, *Science* 140:184-186 (1963), PubMed ID 13953583. https://bionumbers.hms.harvard.edu/bionumber.aspx?id=110319
- Wikipedia: "is variously estimated as taking between 72 and 74 days" (tritium biopsies); another estimate about 120 days; "Including the transport on ductal system, it takes 3 months." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spermatogenesis
- **Verdict:** 64 to 74 days depending on the study. Say "about two and a half months" if used at all.
- **Important honesty point:** Healthline says frequency changes sperm count per ejaculation, not production time: "more frequent ejaculations can lower your sperm count in a single ejaculation." The 74-day figure is not evidence for any "retention benefit". Do not link it to semen-retention claims (see the NoFap file section 3.3). The book can use the number only for "the body replaces what it makes; you are not depleting anything".

## 7. Ideas for chapters

- **02 Why Is It Difficult to Stop.** Augustine's chain: "a lust served, became custom; and custom not resisted, became necessity." Add Wood's 43% habit statistic and Marlatt's wave. Message: it is hard because it is a deeply trained habit and a counterfeit pleasure, not because you are uniquely weak.
- **03 The Flesh.** Bonhoeffer: "a slumbering inclination toward desire, which is both sudden and fierce." Lewis (Great Divorce): "Lust is a poor, weak, whimpering, whispering thing". The flesh is loud, then small.
- **04 The World's Lies.** Screwtape: "He made the pleasures: all our research so far has not enabled us to produce one." Counter "it's harmless / it's natural" with Rogers and Tarwater, and with the Dobson material in the NoFap file. Note also the FitXFearless "quit to attract women" motive in that file.
- **05 Times We Reach For It.** Clear's cue, craving, response, reward. "Make it invisible" for cues. Use Wood: habits are tied to place and context.
- **06 What Am I Giving Up.** Screwtape on pleasure that "diminishes pleasure while increasing desire." Lewis's Four Loves: "a pleasure for which a woman happens to be the necessary piece of apparatus." Lambert: "Lust never has what it wants because it never has enough." The honest answer is: nothing real.
- **07 Time.** Testimonies of years lost: "it's been 6 years now", "since I was 8 years old", Leonard's studies. Owen's "daily work" shows what the habit costs in attention, not hours alone.
- **08 What It's Doing to You.** Lewis: "coming to love the prison"; "indulgence brings fog". Testimonies: Eve ("poisoning my spirit"), Scott ("isolated from real life"), the man who now "hate[s] sex" and does not trust marriage. Piper's missions point is in the NoFap file.
- **10 Willpower.** Owen: "Be killing sin or it will be killing you" and "it must be done by the Spirit." Wood: willpower is not how habits change; reduce friction. Testimony: "I've fasted and I've prayed but is even worst now". Show that grit alone failed these men.
- **11 Cutting Down.** Holy Joys: "Most people cannot conquer lust until they stop masturbating for a time". Lambert: "radical measures". Augustine: "not yet" is a way of cutting down forever.
- **12 Just Once.** Augustine's "only not yet" and "to-morrow, and tomorrow?" Gretel: "I thought I had Kicked the habit but the habit came back."
