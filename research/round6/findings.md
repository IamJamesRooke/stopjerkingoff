# Round 6: dating apps, swiping and the girl on your mind

Gathered 2026-11-19 for the new section in Chapter 15, at James's request ("do research on the forums about it"). Reddit itself could not be searched from here, so these come from a public forum, a newspaper story and a men's advice site. Names are kept out of the book's body, as usual.

## Used in the book

1. **The engaged man who kept swiping.** Blind (teamblind.com), "A strange kind of problem.", 29 Aug 2025, https://www.teamblind.com/post/a-strange-kind-of-problem-hh3lspzo
   - Post: "I'm 35 and have been on hinge for over 8 years with very limited success"; "I met someone 3 months ago at a party and we have decided to get married."; "But the whole fun of swiping on hinge has been so addictive that I'm continuing to do so"; "even though I don't talk to a single person even if they match with me."
   - Reply: "Delete the app immediately before you [...] up a good thing. Don't you get it? You won!"
   - Another reply: "This confirms to me that dating app addiction is a thing, just like porn addiction." (not used)
   - In the book: retold, with the reply's last five words quoted.

2. **The man who swiped yes on every woman.** Daily Mail, 23 Feb 2024, "I needed therapy for my Tinder addiction - at one point I was chatting to 10 women at once" (dailymail.co.uk/health/article-13118139; opening paragraph read on the AITopics copy, https://aitopics.org/doc/technology:C7E73E48). The article says he was "swiping right to every single woman that appeared on his screen", that "At his worst, he was talking to 10 women at once", and that "his aim was to get matches to feel validated and know women found him attractive." These are the paper's words about him, not his own, so the book retells them and quotes nothing. Only the opening paragraph was read: check the full article before leaning on it further.

3. **The one girl.** Joshua Sigafus, "Oneitis", The Adult Man, 9 March 2021 (updated 12 Feb 2024), https://theadultman.com/love-and-lust/oneitis/ : "All he cares about are the feelings he has for her and the longing he feels for her." Quoted in the book as "a man who writes about this". His warning signs include "You Send More Messages Than You Receive" and "The Relationship Involves More 'Hoping' Than 'Experiencing'".

## Background, not used

- r/DatingApps (read through a mirror, no permalinks shown): a man of 29 says the apps "are really starting to seem like a chore" and that opening them "feels almost the same as opening my work email"; another says men were "swiping right to every girl each day and then filtering" their matches.
- PsyPost reported a study linking problematic dating-app use and pornography use to a weaker sense of meaning in romantic love. Not read in full and not used: the book makes no medical or research claims here.
- The phone details in the "one girl" paragraph (checking forty times an hour, rewriting a message five times, knowing when she was last online) are the writer's own picture, not findings.
