# Book bible: *Stop Jerking Off: The Easy Peasy Way to Conquer Lust*

Written 2026-11-17 from a full read of main (commit 3416cf4). Read this instead of the book when editing. Update it after each batch. For problems, see `ISSUES.md`.

## 1. The book in one paragraph

A free, anonymous, Christian-scaffolded self-help book for men, in the spirit of EasyPeasy (itself built on Allen Carr). Thesis: lust gives a man nothing, so once he sees that, stopping is easy and needs no willpower. Lust has three faces: porn, the hand, and bad decisions with women ("thinking with the wrong head"). Frame: a man ruled by lust is a soldier who shows up drunk for duty. He is loved and he is unfit. The book gets him fit. Method: read in order, carry on as normal until the end, then pray the prayer in Ch 27. Free to read, CC BY-SA 4.0. About 69,000 prose words; Ch 8, 30, 5, 13, 10 and 28 are the longest.

## 2. Voice and style rules

- Second person, warm, confident, repetitive on purpose (EasyPeasy device). Short paragraphs. Slogans in CAPS. The odd joke. British spelling (colour, realise, grey, fortnight); the Bible and quoted people stay as written.
- Plain language: one idea per sentence, split anything over about 25 words (quotations and verses excepted), everyday words.
- "I" is for opinions and asides only. No invented autobiography. Stories about people are told about "a man", true on material facts; readable beats hyper-accurate.
- The book is ANONYMOUS. No author name, no pen name, no "I was caught in this". The only credit is CC BY-SA to EasyPeasy (its author is credited as "Hackauthor²" on the Attribution page and in Ch 33; that is EasyPeasy's credit, not ours).
- Scripture: World English Bible, verbatim. Never trim or reword a verse. Other quotes verbatim and short; anything unverifiable becomes a paraphrase. Sources go in each chapter's `<!-- SOURCES -->` comment.
- Named in the body: Bible figures, Augustine, Owen, Chalmers, Lewis, Pascal, Piper, Lambert, Challies, Dobson, Maltz, Bonhoeffer. Everyone else is "one coach", "a pastor", "a psychiatrist who treats porn addiction", "a man on the NoFap forum". Names go in SOURCES, the Appendix and Attribution.
- "Masturbation" is the normal word; "the habit" means masturbation with or without porn; say "lust" when the claim covers all three faces. "Jerking off" is rare and on purpose (title, Ch 9 joke, Intro, Ch 3, Ch 8, Ch 23).
- Respect for women: the fault is the man's thirst, never the woman. "Simping" appears once (Intro).
- No medical claims. Harm is conviction and testimony. No invented studies, stats or quotes. No superpowers, no Karezza.
- "If you're an atheist, play along" appears only in the Intro and the Ch 32 blessing. Elsewhere "if you're not a believer".
- "Carry on as normal" is about private habits. It never permits hurting someone (affair, using a woman, anything illegal).
- Guilt is not the engine. Say the truth about sin, then give grace in the same breath. "Unfit" never means "unloved".
- Never call EasyPeasy a sequel: "spiritual successor", "not an official sequel".

## 3. Key terms

- **Lust**: desire turned into taking. Desire is God's idea and good (noticing beauty, wanting a wife). Lust is the second look, the woman used as material.
- **Three faces / doors / outlets**: porn, the hand, the chase (bad decisions with women). Wording drifts ("faces" in Intro/FAQ, "outlets/doors" in Ch 3, "rooms" in Why This Book Exists); pick "faces".
- **The habit**: masturbation, with or without porn. **MO / PMO**: defined in the Intro.
- **Little Monster**: the faint restless itch ("go on, just do it / look / text her"). It is small and starves fast. **Big Monster**: the lies in your head that make the itch seem important. Kill the Big Monster and the Little one starves. (Ch 30 and the Appendix blur them: the Appendix says the Little Monster "speaks".)
- **The flesh** (Ch 3): the part of a man that wants what it wants, now, for itself. **The world's lies** (Ch 4): the second cause.
- **Willpower Method** (Ch 10, capitalised): any way of quitting that leaves you feeling you sacrificed something. Includes counters, streaks, rations, badges.
- **Ration / cutting down**: any "only on Saturdays / only no screen" deal. Always rejected.
- **Substitute**: anything pushed into the "gap". Always rejected. There is no gap.
- **Just once**: does not exist. It is a chain.
- **The thread**: "A man who can't control his lust can't be trusted." Self-control is a fruit, not a clenched fist.
- **Fit for service / drunk soldier**: loved, enlisted, unfit.
- **Lever**: lust gives others a handle on you (Ch 8).
- **Moment of revelation**: the day the weight lifts (Ch 26). **Flatline**: the temporary flat spell (Ch 21).
- **"YES! I'M FREE!"**: the book's cry. **"Isn't it marvellous?"**: the first-weeks attitude.
- **The final step**: a prayer of confession, not a last session (Ch 27).

## 5. Chapter map

**Intro** (index.Rmd). Promise: you will stop lust, no shame, no grit. Drunk soldier. Self-test list. Three faces, lust vs desire. Faith note. Terms. "Do not skip chapters." *Sets up*: carry on as normal; Ch 1 explains; the soldier returns "near the end".

**1 The Easy Method.** Aim: change how you think. "Carry on as normal" explained. Pascal's bet. Why willpower fails (stopping is not the problem; guilt loads the gun; reasons make it harder). Three questions: what does lust do for me? *Promises*: reasons come off the shelf later (Ch 21); experiment in first weeks; final step is a prayer.

**2 Why Is It Difficult to Stop?** Millions do it, none would start again. Explanations fail. "It's just a habit" is false: it's a master (John 8:34). The trap's design. Two causes. *Sets up*: Ch 3 the flesh, Ch 4 the lies.

**3 The Flesh.** Nothing wrong with the drive. Lust vs desire, three doors. Cue-urge-relief loop. The Little Monster, the alarm, food vs habit, red line, "thinking with the wrong head". Smoker on the plane: the flesh is easy. Self-control as fruit. *Sets up*: Ch 4 lies; Ch 8 "comes back" to the lever.

**4 The World's Lies.** Big Monster. "Natural, harmless, everyone does it" answered fairly (critics granted, doctors on "death grip"). Lies about porn and women. The quiet pulpit, Dobson. The opposite lie (superpowers). "Giving up." *Sets up*: Ch 5.

**5 The Times We Reach for It.** Stress, bed, concentration, relaxation, sleep, energy, dates, loneliness. Each occasion is a lie. David's idle evening. *Promises*: Ch 22/26b finish David.

**6 What Am I Giving Up?** Nothing. The cream and the rash; the void. Paul's ledger. Overlaps Ch 4 "Giving up".

**7 Getting Your Time Back.** The clock: 30 minutes a day sums, the hunt, the chase, the job offer. Parcel of years. *Sets up*: "no farewell session, only a prayer".

**8 What It's Doing to You.** Costs already being paid: drive (the coach's "weak man", turned to grace), focus, confidence, standing before God, marriage, the way you see women, death grip. Walls (Proverbs 25:28). "Nobody's lever." Shadows. *Sets up*: Ch 9 joke; the lever returns in 14, 32.

**9 Advantages of Jerking Off.** An empty page.

**10 The Willpower Method.** How a man gets hooked, the first attempts, why willpower fails, the counter, Romans 7, Owen: only the Spirit. Enjoyment never came into it. Self-control as a quiet door, not a siege.

**11 Beware of Cutting Down.** Rations make it precious; "less is not less"; "willpower for life"; edging; retraining; Romans 13:14.

**12 Just Once.** Just once doesn't exist; the special one; "I'm built this way"; the little foxes.

**13 Types of User.** Why all light users are caught. Nightly, married, streak, porn, "porn-free but still doing it", imagination-only, man led by women, church guy, "I'll stop when I'm married", a week in the life of a ration. Pharisee prayer. "No real types." A word about boys (see Ch 30).

**14 Fantasy and the Thought Life.** Soft porn and the feed, the harem, "look but don't touch" (Matthew 5:28), the woman in the room, taking thoughts captive. Feed as business: the lever.

**15 A Secret Habit?** Secrecy keeps it going. Forums help but use willpower. Into the light. *Promises*: when to tell comes later (Ch 28).

**16 Timing.** Stop when you finish; don't wait; "NOW" vs "carry on"; the sticking plaster; "my life is stressful". Three questions. Augustine: "Why not now?"

**17 Will I Miss It?** No. Don't envy men still in the trap. Pity, pray, hand him the book.

**18 Can I Compartmentalise?** Two rooms are one. What you rehearse. Lewis "medium of self-adoration". One man, no double life.

**19 Avoid False Incentives.** Bribes, superpowers, "to get women", the 90-day badge and pact, "when I'm married". Seek first the Kingdom. Don't announce; hand the book on quietly.

**20 The Easy Way to Stop.** The two instructions: decide never again, and rejoice (repent and receive). One man's last time. Five things. Free the moment you decide. Shame vs motive. Chalmers. If you feel gloomy. Two warnings: don't decide before the end; "first weeks" is not three weeks of pain.

**21 The First Weeks.** Leftover itch and cue triggers (indicator and wipers). Do nothing with an urge. Not a sugar pill. Not a relapse: desire, morning erections, wet dreams, flat spell. Try-it-and-see (retention, honestly). Pass the book to a mate.

**22 Just One Little Look.** The little look feeds both monsters. Job's covenant.

**23 Will It Be Harder for Me?** Church leader, decades of use, student and single man. Two causes of failure: outside trigger, bad day.

**24 Substitutes.** Edging, touching, porn "instead", scroll, food, a woman "instead". Near-beer of lust. Pursue (2 Timothy 2:22).

**25 Should I Avoid Temptation?** Phase One panic, Phase Two future. No "way back". Phone in the bedroom: no fortress, no dare. Joseph vs Samson. Go out and be social.

**26 The Moment of Revelation.** What it is; days not weeks; the three-week trap; don't wait for it.

**26b Fit for Service** (unnumbered). Out of the fight (David, enemy, Nehemiah). Drunk on duty (Uriah, Ai, sober first, grace trains). "Here I am. Send me!" *Pays off*: Intro soldier. *Sets up*: Ch 27.

**27 The Final Step.** Two essentials; vow vs yes-yes; the prayer (porn, hand, women, "show me the man I am to go back for"); let it ring (Maltz); you are already free; final warning, "not once".

**28 Feedback.** The man who goes back in; the cellar door. Fears; the misreadings ("fell, am I back to zero?", tell your wife or pastor); the checklist of seven; more questions (wet dream, body with wife, sleep).

**29 Help Those on the Sinking Ship.** Pity, don't envy; how to help; don't kick; fences don't free; your son needs an explanation.

**30 Advice to Non-users.** Wives, parents, pastors, a note on women, then the user's own sections: slipping, the bully, the pebble parody, porn and sex, where we leave EasyPeasy (no last session), help end the silence, final warning.

**31 The Instructions.** Eleven lines, verses, "YES! I'M FREE!".

**32 Now.** Today only. Where to now: the cleared room, nobody's lever, back in the fight. Pass it on. Blessing (Numbers 6).
