# Overnight round, 2026-11-21: every change, before and after

Five things were done: a read-aloud pass, a fact and quote audit, a plain-English pass, the fixes from Method Check 3 and Pastor Review 3, and the safe items from Reader Report 4. Chapters are in reading order.

## Summary

- **Read-aloud:** run-together quotes got speaker tags (The Best Liar, Whose Voice, the bleach forum in If You Slip). A spoken reference was removed from the end of Can You Be Trusted?.
- **Fact and quote audit:** every attributed quote and every modern fact was matched to its source note. Nothing was found shaky, so nothing was rewritten or cut.
- **Plain English:** about thirty idioms were swapped for plain words. 'Mope' is now explained at its first use. House words stayed (pipsqueak, American spellings).
- **Method Check 3:** all nine fixes applied. The largest: the non-believer line after Chalmers no longer asks for a replacement love, Just Once no longer reads as saying No every time, and the verses are no longer for answering the Little Monster.
- **Pastor Review 3:** all fixes applied. 'Partner' became 'wife' in 31 places (this reverses an earlier standing choice of James's, so it is his to undo). The Ahab chapter's opening was fixed.
- **Reader Report 4:** the marriage line keeps its position and is said more warmly at the Final Step. 'But I'm Going to Marry Her' has a new section, 'If you already share a bed', for the man who lives with his girlfriend. The Empty House no longer lists the fiancée beside affairs.
- **Not done, on purpose:** no 'skip this chapter' permission (it would contradict the do-not-skip rule; the chapters say who they are for and ask the reader to read on). No crisis phone line (Evergreen rule). No softening of the four method lines. David is already told in full only once, in A City Without Walls.
- **Left for James:** repeat-slip help, more for the chaser after he stops, the secular route at The Easy Way to Stop, and whether The People Beside Him (2,097 words) should be split.

> ## If you already share a bed {-}
> 
> Perhaps you are long past this line with the woman you love. Perhaps you live together, and have for years. You haven't ruined anything. You start from the same place as every other man in these pages.
> 
> But you can't settle this one alone in your head, because she is in it. So talk to her, in daylight, and not at bedtime. Tell her what you have seen about yourself. Tell her it is no judgment on her, and that you aren't leaving. Then tell her what you want: to stop taking from her and to start promising to her.
> 
> After that, the two of you have real choices. You can bring the wedding forward. Plenty of couples have married within a month, with ten guests. Or you can keep the bed for the wedding from here on. That isn't simple under one roof, so some men move out for the months between. If she doesn't share your conviction, listen to her. Don't preach, and don't make her the problem. The line doesn't move. How the two of you get behind it is yours to work out together.
> 
> Only one choice is closed. Don't tell yourself you'll sort it out later and go on as before. "Later" is the Little Monster's favorite word.
