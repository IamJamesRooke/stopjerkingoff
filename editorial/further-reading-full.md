## Further reading

Everything below was drawn on in writing this book. I've said honestly where an item and this book disagree.

### The books this one grew from

- **[*EasyPeasy*](https://easypeasymethod.org/)** by Hackauthor². It's free, and it's the free online book this one is modelled on, chapter for chapter. This book grew out of it. Read it too.
- **Allen Carr, *The Easy Way to Stop Smoking*.** The method comes from here. It's worth reading even if you've never smoked, just to watch the method work on a different trap.

### Old books that have lasted

- **Augustine, *Confessions*, Book 8.** He prays "not yet", hears a child's voice in a garden, and is free the same day. It was written sixteen centuries ago and it's the best account of a divided will that I know. E.B. Pusey's translation is in the public domain and [free to read online](https://faculty.georgetown.edu/jod/augustine/Pusey/book08).
- **John Owen, *The Mortification of Sin* (1656).** The classic on putting sin to death. Owen sounds like the opposite of this book, since he calls the fight a daily work. But he insists it can only be done by the Spirit and never by grit, so he's really on our side. The prose is hard going and worth the effort.
- **Thomas Chalmers, "The Expulsive Power of a New Affection".** It's a single sermon, and it explains better than anything else why a desire leaves only when a greater love arrives. It's [free online](https://www.preceptaustin.org/expulsive-power-of-a-new-affection).

### C.S. Lewis

- ***Mere Christianity*, Book III, chapter 5, "Sexual Morality".** It's short and sane, and there's no panic in it.
- ***The Screwtape Letters*.** A senior devil explains temptation to a junior one. You'll recognise the Big Monster's whole method, including pleasures that shrink while the demand for them grows.
- ***The Four Loves*, the chapter on Eros.** It's about the difference between wanting a sensation and loving a person.
- **His 1956 letter to Keith Masson**, printed in the third volume of his *Collected Letters*. This is where the "harem of imaginary brides" comes from. I've only seen the parts that others quote, and those alone are worth a library trip.

### More recent Christian writing

- **Dietrich Bonhoeffer, *Temptation*.** A brief book about how suddenly desire wakes, and whose strength a man stands in when it does.
- **John Piper, ["Missions and Masturbation"](https://www.desiringgod.org/articles/missions-and-masturbation)** and ["Strategies for Fighting Sexual Sin"](https://www.desiringgod.org/messages/strategies-for-fighting-sexual-sin). Piper is a fighter, and his language is all battle. Read him for his seriousness and his hope. Don't let the battle language talk you back into the Willpower Method.
- **Heath Lambert, *Finally Free*.** It's written about pornography, and grace is its subject from start to finish. It applies to the hand as well.
- **Tim Challies, ["Self-Centered Sex"](https://www.challies.com/articles/self-centered-sex-part-2/).** A plain-spoken pastor on why the habit isolates a man.
- **Brittany Allen, ["Debunking 4 Justifications for Masturbation"](https://www.thegospelcoalition.org/article/debunking-justifications-masturbation/)**. A careful answer to "but is it really a sin?"
- **Johnathan Arnold, ["Is Masturbation a Sin?"](https://holyjoys.org/is-masturbation-sin/)**. He says to stop at once and completely, and we agree.
- **Luke Gilkerson, ["Three Steps to Kick the Habit of Masturbation"](https://www.covenanteyes.com/blog/three-steps-to-kick-the-habit-of-masturbation/).** His advice to find the lies you believe and replace them with truth is this book in miniature. Under the article are years of readers' comments, and many of the testimonies quoted here came from them. One difference: the site that hosts it sells accountability software, and this book doesn't ask you to use any.
- **Craig Groeschel, ["When the Urge Is Too Strong"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7xzzJ6uLnbQ)** (a sermon). The M&Ms in Chapter 25 and the "was it worth it?" story in Chapter 14 are his. He is funny, and honest about himself.
- **Mike Novotny, ["What Does God and the Bible Say About Masturbation?"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hcra9n8aMyg)** (a talk). A pastor who admits straight away that the Bible never names the habit, and then shows why that doesn't settle it.
- **Ezekiel ("Brother Zeke"), ["How Mast\*rbation Had Me Bound but God"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mppw_mrhlIY)** (a testimony). Caught at ten, free as a man. Several of the best lines in this book are his, or come from the comments under his video. His tradition speaks more about spirits than this book does.
- **Jason Evert, ["Freedom from Lust"](https://bustedhalo.com/podcasts/jason-evert-on-freedom-from-lust/)** (a podcast interview). A Catholic speaker on chastity as the thing that makes love possible.

### Voices from outside the church

- **FitXFearless** (Bismarck Ebiweh) on YouTube. He's blunt and energetic, and he's honest about what the habit costs a young man. We've quoted him gratefully. We don't follow him when he sells quitting as a way to attract women, or when he describes semen as a man's energy source. Take what's honest from him and leave those two.
- **Other coaches and channels.** Captain Sinbad, QuitByHealing and Nick Jones on YouTube were all drawn on. QuitByHealing's advice on urges, to notice them and do nothing, is the nearest thing to this book's method that I found outside EasyPeasy. Captain Sinbad describes the first weeks well, but he recommends turning lust into ambition, and this book doesn't.
- **Dr Doug Weiss, ["The Real Way to Quit Masturbating for Good"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vmlk6YdWLuY).** A Christian therapist with decades of clients behind him. He is good on secrecy and on abuse. He recommends rewards and punishments, and Chapter 19 explains why we don't.
- **The NoFap forum.** Many of the men quoted in this book wrote there. See the caution below.
- **Phineas 808, ["16 Principles of Recovery"](https://www.yourbrainonporn.com/tools-for-change-recovery-from-porn-addiction/rebooting-advice-observations-from-successful-rebooters/16-principles-of-recovery/).** A short list by a man who got out. His line about becoming "a man who does not" is as close to this book's method as anything on the forums.
- **Maxwell Maltz, *Psycho-Cybernetics*.** The ringing telephone in Chapter 27 is his.
- **James Clear, *Atomic Habits*,** and **Wendy Wood, *Good Habits, Bad Habits*.** Two plain accounts of how habits hang on cues, if you'd like the everyday mechanics.
- **Cleveland Clinic, ["Are 'Wet Dreams' Normal and Healthy?"](https://health.clevelandclinic.org/wet-dreams)** For the reader who's still worried after Chapter 28.

A caution about forums in general. You'll find real honesty there, and you'll also find day counters, miracle claims and men on their fortieth reset. Read the testimonies if they help you. Leave the counters where they are.
