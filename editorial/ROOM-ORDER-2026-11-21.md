# Room-by-room reorder, 2026-11-21

James, after listening to chapter 6: the opening still front-loads, and names every problem at once. He approved a new rule and left the rest to the Author's judgment: nothing arrives before the reader needs it, and the rooms come in the order an addict retreats through them. Telling a man "no sex before marriage" too soon gives him fear; by the time each smaller room has fallen, he will want it and see that it makes sense.

The book before this change is kept on the branch backup/pre-room-order-2026-11-21. Text taken out is in editorial/PARKING.md.

## The new order

The contents list is in /mnt/project-files/easy-peasy/contents-2026-11-21.md. Three chapters moved:

- **Can You Be Trusted?** was chapter 2. It is now chapter 21, after the costs and just before A City Without Walls, where David proves it.
- **The Back Door** (the hand without porn) now leads the room chapters, as chapter 33.
- **Where the Line Is** was chapter 5. It is now chapter 40, just before But I'm Going to Marry Her.

The reader now reaches The Little Monster at chapter 7 (it was 9), with the Introduction and three chapters before the proof starts.

## What changed in the text

**Introduction.** The line "One pastor who writes honestly about masturbation calls it 'a regular practice of most single men'..." is cut (James never liked it). The short note for women readers moved here from Can You Be Trusted?.

**The Button (1).** New ending, taken from the old chapter 2: "So how do you get out? It begins with the strangest promise anyone has made you about this. It will be easy."

**The Easy Method (2).** Gains the EasyPeasy and Allen Carr credit (one paragraph) and the faith note (two short paragraphs, with Romans 8:1), placed just before Paul is quoted.

**The Empty House (3).** The list of five ways is gone from here. It keeps: "Everything he gets, he gets with what you lend him: your eyes, your mind and your hand. From some men he has borrowed more than that, and we'll come to it." New paragraph, the retreat warning: "One warning about him. He never leaves when you shut a room. He moves down the hall. You will hear it in your own voice: 'Fine, not that. But surely this is all right.' When you hear that sentence, smile. That is him, changing rooms. This book follows him room by room, and it goes further than you expect. You don't have to agree to anything in advance. Judge each room when we get there." New ending into Why Is It Difficult to Stop?: "You aren't stupid. You have wanted him out for years. So why does it feel so hard?"

**The Flesh, The Little Monster.** "Lust is fed five ways" became "in more ways than one", and "fed five ways" became "fed many ways", so the five are not named before their rooms.

**Both Sides of the Rope (20).** New last line: "One cost is left. It is the one a man least likes to hear said aloud."

**Can You Be Trusted? (21).** Now only the monk, the drunk soldier, the hard sentence and the house-cat paragraph. New ending: "None of this is new. Three thousand years ago it brought down a king that no army could beat."

**A City Without Walls (22).** "Here the thread that runs through this book comes out into the open" became "So say the hard sentence once more, with David's name on it."

**At Least I'm Not Him (32).** New ending: "And every 'at least' is a room he hasn't opened yet. Remember the warning about the squatter. Shut one room on him and he doesn't leave. He moves down the hall. So follow him. Start with the man who has quit porn and is proud of it, and listen to what he says next."

**The rooms (33 to 41).** Each one now opens or closes on his excuse:

- The Back Door opens: "Fine. No porn. But the hand on its own is natural." It introduces the test: "Is this a gift, with a person at the other end of it? Or is it a hit I'm taking?" It ends: "And now the squatter picks up his blanket and moves down the hall. 'Fine. Nothing on the screen, and hands off. But what goes on in my own head is nobody's business.'"
- Fantasy and the Thought Life opens: "This room has no porn in it and no hand. It has a phone, and a head." The test is used once: "There is nobody at the other end, so it is a hit."
- Mothers, Sisters, Daughters ends: "Those are the rooms a man can sit in by himself. Now the squatter does something new. He borrows your feet, and walks out into company. 'I'm only being friendly.'"
- The Man Led by a Woman: "It is a hit dressed up as a gift."
- The Collector and the Customer ends: "And here the squatter makes his last move, and it is his best. 'Fine. Not strangers. But a woman I love is different.' Before that room, one question has to be settled, and you have been circling it for a long time. Where exactly is the line?"
- Where the Line Is opens: "You have followed him through every room but one. Look, think, touch, chase, take." The five are named together here for the first time. The marriage line is word for word as before. The section on the right woman is parked, because the next chapter is about that man. New ending: "One room is left, and it is the hardest in the house. The man in it isn't using a stranger. He has found the right woman, and he means to keep her."
- But I'm Going to Marry Her ends: "That is every room in the house."

## What did not change

The four method lines, the marriage line, the Final Step, the harm caveat and the get-help note in the Introduction, and every quote and verse that stayed.
