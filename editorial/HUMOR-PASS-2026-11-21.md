# Humor pass, 2026-11-21: every change, before and after

The plan is in humor-pass-plan.md. The aim: a man laughs in the first two minutes and sees that his problem is smaller than he thought. Chapters are in reading order.

## What I found

The whole book was read again from the first line. Most chapters already carried a light moment from the earlier passes (the fishing button, the toast speech, the mashed potatoes, the man looking for his shoes, the Bleachstronauts), so they were left alone. The work went where the book was still heavy or where the new picture had to be threaded in.

## What changed

- **Page one.** The squatter now appears in the Introduction, with the line "You have been fighting a dragon, and there is no dragon." Straight after it comes James's line: save your grit, don't let this battle define you, evict him and get on with your life.
- **The safety list** ("Before you go on") is word for word the same and still in the Introduction, but it now comes after "Is this you?". It is shown below as one move.
- **The martyr** gets his full answer in The Willpower Method (the medal, the gravestone).
- **One picture, not two.** The Little Monster chapter says outright that he is the squatter. A Master, Not a Habit now admits the joke of his size.
- **Callbacks at the turning points:** the squatter's stomach rumbling (Good News, Twice), the armor for a squatter (The World's Lies), courage saved for the service (Fit for Service), a pest and not the story of your life (Let It Ring), the windows opened and what the grit was for (Now).
- **Smaller than you think:** Timing no longer calls this "the worst thing that ever happened" to a man. "The sinister trap" is now "The quiet trap".

## Left alone on purpose

The grave places (the safety list's wording, abuse, Uriah's death, abortion, the prayers, the mercy in If You Slip), every verse and quote, the four hard method lines, the five names, the marriage line, the order and the numbers.
