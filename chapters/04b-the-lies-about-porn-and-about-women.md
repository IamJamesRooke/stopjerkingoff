# The Lies About Porn and About Women {#the-lies-about-porn-and-about-women}

The world has a second set of lies for the screen, and a third for real women. Take the screen first.

**"Porn is just entertainment. Nobody gets hurt."** Somebody always does. You can't know whether the woman on the screen wanted to be there. You can't check her age, and you can't check whether she agreed. The sites haven't checked either. In 2020 a New York Times report found videos of rape and of underage girls on the biggest porn site in the world. The big card companies stopped handling its payments. Within days the site took down nearly ten million videos, because it had never checked who uploaded them.

Here is one case out of many. A company in California promised young women that their videos would never go online, and then put them on the free sites, where they were watched millions of times. In 2025 its owner was sentenced to twenty-seven years in prison for sex trafficking.

Millions of ordinary men watched those videos. Not one of them thought he was watching a crime. And the man who watches is not a bystander. He is the customer. The video was made for him, and his visit pays for the next one. Nobody told him. He was sold it as harmless entertainment, and so were you. It never was.

Whoever she is, she is somebody's daughter. And when it is her own choice, she has been told a lie of her own: that her worth is her body, and that men's hunger is a way to make a living. What ruins the man who watches ruins the woman who is watched.

**"At least I've never paid for sex."** You have, though nobody told you so. In porn a woman is paid to have sex so that a man can get off. That is prostitution, with a camera between her and you. The man at the brothel door hands her the money. The man at the screen pays through the site, with a card or with his eyes, because the site sells his attention. Think of cocaine and crack. Cocaine was the rich man's drug. Crack is the same drug made cheap, and sold on every corner. Porn is the crack. It is the brothel made cheap, open all night, and sold in every pocket.

**"It isn't a real woman, so nobody is hurt."** This lie arrived in the 2020s, when computers learned to make pictures and to talk. The picture was drawn, or a computer made it, or the girlfriend is a program. First, somebody often is hurt. Computers were soon being used to put real women's faces into porn they never made. One 2023 study counted nearly a hundred thousand of these fake videos online. Almost all of them were porn, and almost all the faces belonged to real women who never agreed to it. Second, even when no woman exists at all, you do. The thing being trained is your own heart, and it learns the same lesson from a drawing as from a photograph.

Now the lies about real women.

**"A real man gets as many women as he can."** That is the oldest lie of all. It makes a man's worth depend on being wanted, which is exactly what makes him easy to lead. A real man is one a woman can trust. Count how many of the men who live by the other rule you would trust with your sister.

**"It's only a little fun. She knew what it was."** Then why did you have to tell yourself so? Using a person is still using her when she agrees to it. Paul's rule, in his first letter to the Christians in Thessalonica, is short: "that no one should take advantage of and wrong a brother or sister in this matter". You already knew that. It is why it never felt like fun the next morning.

She has been lied to as well. She was told that sleeping with a man and expecting nothing from him is freedom. She was told that a pill makes it safe. She was told that if a child comes anyway, the child's life can be ended, as though that would cost her nothing. Nobody told her the price, and nobody asked what she was worth. If you were part of a choice like that, it is forgiven the same way as everything else in this book. The grief is real, and there is help for it.

You don't have to take that from a church. In 2022 a British journalist named Louise Perry, who had worked at a rape crisis center, wrote a book arguing that the sexual revolution mostly served men who want sex without ties. One of her chapters is called "Consent Is Not Enough". Studies are mixed, and many people say they felt fine at the time. But the regret shows when researchers ask. In one study of Canadian students in 2012, about three in four of those who had sex with no commitment said they had regretted it at some time, the men as well as the women.

So "she agreed" settles nothing. Men like to complain about women who have been with too many men. They never ask who those men were. If you slept with her and left, you were one of them. You helped to make the very woman you say you would never marry.

None of this is here to add to your guilt. It is here to take away the last excuse. You were lied to, and so was she. You don't need any of it, and it was never harmless.

One liar is left, and he is the most persuasive of them all. You have known him all your life.
