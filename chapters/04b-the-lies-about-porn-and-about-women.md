# The Lies About Porn {#the-lies-about-porn-and-about-women}

The world has a second set of lies, for the screen.

**"Porn is just entertainment. Nobody gets hurt."** Somebody always does. You can't know whether the woman on the screen wanted to be there. You can't check her age. The sites haven't checked either. In 2020 a New York Times report found videos of rape and of underage girls on the biggest porn site in the world. The big card companies stopped handling its payments. Within days the site took down nearly ten million videos, because it had never checked who uploaded them.

Take one case out of many. A company in California promised young women that their videos would never go online. Then it put them on the free sites, where they were watched millions of times. In 2025 its owner was sentenced to twenty-seven years in prison for sex trafficking.

Ordinary men watched those videos. Not one of them thought he was watching a crime. And the man who watches is not a bystander. He is the customer. The video was made for him, and his visit pays for the next one. Nobody told him. He was sold it as harmless entertainment, and so were you. It never was.

Whoever she is, she is somebody's daughter. And when it is her own choice, she has been told a lie of her own. It says her worth is her body, and men's hunger is a way to make a living. What ruins the man who watches ruins the woman who is watched.

**"At least I've never paid for sex."** You have, though nobody told you so. In porn a woman is paid to have sex so that a man can have his release. That is prostitution, with a camera between her and you. The man at the brothel door hands her the money. The man at the screen pays through the site, with a card or with his eyes, because the site sells his attention. Porn is the brothel made cheap, open all night, and sold in every pocket.

**"It isn't a real woman, so nobody is hurt."** This lie arrived in the 2020s, when computers learned to make pictures and to talk. The picture was drawn, or a computer made it, or the girlfriend is a program. First, somebody often is hurt. Computers were soon putting real women's faces into videos they never made. One 2023 study counted nearly a hundred thousand of these fake videos online. Almost all of them were porn, and almost all the faces belonged to real women who never agreed to it. Second, even when no woman exists at all, you do. The thing being trained is your own heart, and it learns the same lesson from a drawing as from a photograph.

None of this is here to add to your guilt. It is here to take away the last excuse. You were lied to, and so was the woman on the screen. It was never harmless.

Those are the lies. Now look at some of the men who believed them.
