# Porn, and Sex {#porn-and-sex}

**"What about porn?"**

It went with the rest. Not afterward, and not gradually. Porn and masturbation are one trap, and each feeds the other. Your No covered both. This book was inspired by [EasyPeasy](https://easypeasymethod.org/), which deals with porn alone.

**"What about sex?"**

This book has nothing against sex. It's against a counterfeit of it, and the counterfeit is everywhere.

Look at what we've all been sold. Porn teaches a man that women are for hire. The culture tells him he isn't a real man until he's slept with dozens of them. The same machine works on women from the other side. A phone full of likes teaches a girl that her body is what she has to offer. It teaches her that being looked at is the same as being loved. Men are taught to shop, and women are taught to put themselves on display. Both are lied to, and both come away emptier. We've been told this is freedom. Look at the men and women it has produced, and ask whether they look free.

You already know where the line is. Sex belongs inside marriage. The letter to the Hebrews, in the New Testament, says, "Let marriage be held in honor among all, and let the bed be undefiled". That isn't because sex is dirty. It's because sex is strong. It ties two people together, and lust wants the tie without the promise. Within marriage, sex is a gift, and a married man who has stopped the habit shouldn't hold back from his wife.

If you're not a believer, the test in your pocket gives the same answer. A gift, inside a promise already made? Or a hit?

If you've already lived the other way, nobody here is piling on guilt. The man who wrote this book is no angel. Christians aren't the people who got it right. They're the people who finally admitted they couldn't, and asked for help. That's the heart of the faith. Look honestly and you find you aren't as good as you thought. Nobody is, and nobody gets there alone.

So if you're single, keep it for marriage. What you keep isn't lost. It is saved for her. Don't go looking for somebody to use in place of your hand. That is the same lust with another person in it. And if you're carrying a past, it's forgiven the same way as everything else in this book. You confess it, and you start again.

That leaves the question nobody likes to ask out loud. What if you fall?
