# The First Touch {#the-first-touch}

It is morning. You wake up hard, as a healthy man does. Nobody is home, and you have half an hour.

You don't decide anything. Your hand drifts down, only to adjust. One touch. Then a second, a little longer. It feels good, and better if it has been a while. Somewhere around the third, the question is over. You never said yes. You are only a man who doesn't want to stop.

That is how it nearly always happens. Nobody sits up in bed and announces a plan.

## The needle {-}

Ask where a heroin addict makes his decision. It isn't when the drug hits. By then nobody is left to decide. It is before the needle goes in. Once it is in, it is in. A man who has swallowed the pill can't unswallow it on the stairs.

The first touch is the needle going in. Everything after it is the drug talking. So a man is free in one place only, and that place is before.

## The free sample {-}

Picture the lie as it is at that moment. It is a dealer on a street corner, and this morning he isn't selling. He is giving.

"First one's free. Just a taste. You don't have to buy anything."

You would laugh and keep walking. Everybody knows what the free one is for. The first touch is the free sample. It costs nothing and commits you to nothing, and that is exactly how it works.

## Just one chip {-}

The makers of Lay's potato chips once ran a famous slogan. "Betcha can't eat just one." It may be the most honest advertisement ever written. They weren't boasting about the taste. They were telling you how the product works.

The first chip is not a small helping of chips. It is what opens the bag.

## Fasting is easier than dieting {-}

Any man who has tried both knows that it is easier to fast than to diet.

The dieter has to stop in the middle of the meal, with the food in his mouth, a hundred times a week. The man who fasts decides once, before he is hungry, and then there is nothing to argue about.

"A little, and then I'll stop" is a diet. It is the hardest thing you could ask of yourself. None is the easy version.

## Where the choice is {-}

This is no rule to grit your teeth over. It is a map of where the choice is. The choice is in the first two seconds, while you are still wholly yourself. It costs nothing there.

So the instruction fits in four words, and your grandmother could have given it. Stop touching your junk.

When you wake up hard, it is plumbing. It is not a summons. Get up and make the coffee.

The lie has one more trick. It points at another man and says, "Look at him. He has it under control."
