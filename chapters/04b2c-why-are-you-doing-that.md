# Why Are You Doing That? {#why-are-you-doing-that}

You're scrolling. Your cousin's dog. A meme. A guy selling knives.

Then her.

A girl you sort of knew in high school. She's in the bathroom mirror, back arched like she pulled a muscle, phone held high, lips pushed out like she's trying to kiss a fish. Under it, the caption:

- "Carpe diem." That's Latin for "seize the day."
- "Living my best life."
- "Blessed."

You know what used to happen next. The second look. The zoom. Maybe a tap to see the other nine pictures. That was a free snack for the Little Monster, the pathetic freeloader in the back room upstairs. He'd stop banging long enough to lick his fingers, and then he'd bang for more.

Here's a different reaction. Try it next time and see how it feels.

**Why are you doing that?**

## Just be normal {-}

Look at the picture again, but this time look at her, not at the body. What is she doing?

She's standing in a bathroom, alone, pulling a strange face at a phone. She's asking strangers to tell her she's worth something. The caption says she's seizing the day. The picture says she's spent an hour on it.

It's not sexy. It's sad. And if you're honest, it's a little weird.

So say what any decent friend would say. Say it in your head, kindly.

- Why are you doing that?
- Just be nice.
- Just be normal.
- Put some clothes on.
- Find a nice guy. Have a family.
- Stop doing weird stuff for attention.

It's what her grandmother would say. It's what her father would say if he saw it. It's what you'd say to your own sister.

The same question works on the guy flexing shirtless in the gym mirror. Why are you doing that, man? Just be normal.

## Picture him trying it {-}

Here's a test that never fails. Take any pose that's meant to make you hungry, and give it to the Little Monster.

Picture him in front of the mirror. Lips pushed out. Belly sucked in, then popping right back out. One hip cocked. Hair slicked down with something you don't want to ask about. A green stink cloud rising off him like steam off a swamp. He still smells like cheese left to rot in a car in midsummer.

![](images/illustrations/why-monster-duck-face-selfie.png){.illustration width=80%}

That's the pose with the filter off. The same move, the same pout, the same begging for eyes. On her it's dressed up. On him you can see it for what it is.

![](images/illustrations/why-monster-pinup.png){.illustration width=80%}

## She lives in the dirty house too {-}

Don't blame her for your reaction. She grew up in the same house you did. The same stages taught her that her body is her best feature and her attention is her paycheck. The likes are her own little monster, and he bangs on her floor just like yours bangs on yours. Every heart under the picture is one more meal.

Let's be fair the other way too. Some women do this on purpose, for money, and they know what it does to men. That's on them. It still doesn't make the man who keeps looking any less of a fool. You can't control her. You can control whether you pay her with your eyes.

She's somebody's daughter. Somewhere there's a father who'd give anything for her to put the phone down. If you pray, pray for her. If you don't, at least don't feed it. Either way, scroll on.

Notice what just happened. A minute ago she was material. Now she's a person, and a little bit of a sad one. Lust can't survive that switch. It needs her to be a picture. Pity sees a girl in a bathroom who wants to be loved and is going about it all wrong.

Once you've seen it, you can't unsee it. The pout looks silly. The caption looks like a cry for attention. The Little Monster stops getting his snack, and you didn't have to fight anybody.

You just asked a question.

It works on the woman in the video too. She's somebody's daughter, and someone got paid to point a camera at her. Ask her the same question, and watch what it does to the Little Monster's dinner.

![](images/illustrations/why-scroll-on.png){.illustration width=80%}

One question is left. Who keeps putting it in front of you?
