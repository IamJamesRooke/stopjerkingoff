# Who Is Telling You This {#who-is-telling-you-this}

This book is about you. But if I'm going to tell you the habit has no benefits at all, you should know how I know.

## The awkward boy {-}

I was the awkward boy who couldn't talk to girls. Never went to a school dance. Who'd even want me?

At sixteen I found masturbation, and it became my drug. Several times a day. At twenty-one I got fast internet, and the porn came with it. Nobody would've guessed.

When I got my first girlfriend, I couldn't finish with her. My body had been trained on a screen. A real woman was in my bed, and I kept watching porn.

It cost me three relationships, an engagement and a marriage.

## Thirteen ways to be anxious {-}

Then I went the other way. I chased woman after woman, just to run up the count. I spent my nights in bed with the phone, swiping and sexting. When I liked one, I checked for her texts all day. To calm my nerves, I kept a dozen others talking as backups. That's not a man with options. That's a man with thirteen ways to be anxious.

Then I started paying for it. I'd love to tell you I was lonely. I wasn't. Getting dates wasn't the problem. I wanted one thing, and once I got it, I lost interest in the woman. Paying seemed more honest. It was cheaper, too, and faster.

Men like me have a saying. You don't pay her for the sex. You pay her to leave.

I used to think that was clever. Now I hear what it says. That's a man talking about a human being like a rental car. The habit taught me to talk like that.

And none of it was ever enough.

## Men are sedated {-}

The habit's one sedative among many. Look at one ordinary evening.

A man comes home tired and pours a drink to take the edge off. Dinner's a burger and fries out of a paper bag, then a whole sleeve of cookies. He games till midnight. He scrolls for an hour. He finishes on a porn site.

Another man's evening has weed in it, or a betting app. The drug changes. The job's the same. Every one of them is sold as taking the edge off. What it takes off is *his* edge, the part of a man that gets things done. None of it's a crime. All of it's a sedative.

Social media does something extra. It leaves him with a knot in his stomach, and he can't say why. It leaves him half sure nothing matters anyway. Then he checks the news and the price of a house, and decides the game is rigged.

He's not entirely wrong. But a sedated man can't fix a rigged game.

## One drug {-}

I couldn't clear every drug off that list. I wanted to be cured of one. Lust.

I'd fought it for years, mostly with willpower. A free book, [*The Easy Peasy Way to Quit Porn*](https://easypeasymethod.org/), kept me clean for almost a year. I went back, because I didn't do what it said. That's on me, not the book.

I looked for a book like it that went past porn to lust itself. Couldn't find one. So I made this one, for one reader. Me. Then I put it out for anyone to take, because I'm not the only one.

I've been most of the men you'll meet in this book. I gave the habit every chance to pay me back, and it never did. With my record, I'm the last man who could sell you a benefit.

That's enough about me.

## Where the method comes from {-}

I didn't invent this way of quitting. A man named Allen Carr did, for smokers. His book, *The Easy Way to Stop Smoking*, has helped a huge number of people put down cigarettes without a fight. The free book that helped me took Carr's method and turned it on porn. This book borrows from both and takes the method all the way to lust itself. The credit's theirs. Any mistakes are mine.

## If you believe, and if you don't {-}

The best pictures of this trap are in the Bible, so this book quotes it. If you don't believe, read it like an old book that turns out to know you. When you hear God or sin mentioned, take it as your conscience talking. If you hold another faith, bring it with you.

If you're a Christian, hear one verse early. Romans 8:1 says, "There is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus". When this book says "sin", it's telling you the truth. It isn't beating you.

Paul, the apostle who wrote much of the New Testament, told the Christians in Rome to "be transformed by the renewing of your mind". He didn't say try harder. "Be transformed" is something done *to* you, as your mind is made new. A believer will say God does it. If you're not one, call it changing your mind. Either way, it starts with seeing clearly.

So start with the Little Monster. If he does nothing for you, why has every attempt to get rid of him failed? The best answer anyone's given is two thousand years old. It's a story about a house.
