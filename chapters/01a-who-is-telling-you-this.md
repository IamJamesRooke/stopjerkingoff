# Who Is Telling You This {#who-is-telling-you-this}

This book is about you. But a man who says the habit has no benefits at all should tell you how he knows.

## The awkward boy {-}

I was the awkward boy who couldn't talk to girls. I never went to a school dance. Who would even want me?

At sixteen I found masturbation, and it became my drug. Several times a day. At twenty-one I got fast internet, and the porn came with it. Nobody would have guessed.

When I got my first girlfriend, I couldn't finish with her. My body had been trained on a screen. A real woman was in my bed, and I kept watching porn.

It cost me three relationships, an engagement and a marriage.

## Thirteen ways to be anxious {-}

After that I went the other way. I chased woman after woman, just to run up the count. I spent my nights in bed with the phone, swiping and sexting. When I liked one, I checked my phone for her texts all day. To ease the nerves, I kept a dozen other women talking as backups. That is not a man with options. That is a man with thirteen ways to be anxious.

Then I started paying for it. I would like to tell you I was lonely. I wasn't. Dates were not the problem. I wanted one thing. Once I got it, I lost interest in the woman. Paying seemed more honest. It was also cheaper, and it took less time.

Men like me have a saying. You don't pay her for the sex. You pay her to leave.

I used to think that was a clever line. Now I hear what it says. That is a man talking about a human being like a rental car. The habit taught me to talk like that.

And none of it was ever enough.

## Men are sedated {-}

The habit is one sedative among many. Look at one ordinary evening.

A man comes home tired and pours a drink to take the edge off. Dinner is a burger and fries out of a paper bag, then a sleeve of cookies. He plays a game until midnight. He scrolls for an hour. He finishes on a porn site.

Another man's evening has weed in it, or a betting app. The drug changes. The job is the same. Each one is sold as taking the edge off. What it takes off is his edge, the part of a man that gets things done. None of it is a crime. All of it is a sedative.

Social media does something extra. It leaves him with a knot in his stomach, and he can't say why. It leaves him half sure that nothing matters anyway. Then he looks at the news and the price of a house, and he decides the game is rigged.

He is not entirely wrong. But a sedated man can't fix a rigged game.

## One drug {-}

I couldn't clear every drug off that list. I wanted to be cured of one. Lust.

I had fought it for years, mostly with willpower. A free book, [*The Easy Peasy Way to Quit Porn*](https://easypeasymethod.org/), kept me clean for almost a year. I went back, because I hadn't done what it said. That one is on me, not the book.

I looked for a book like it that went past porn to lust itself. I couldn't find one. So I made this book, for one reader. Me. Then I put it out for anyone to take, because I am not the only one.

I have been most of the men you'll meet in this book. I gave the habit every chance to pay me back, and it never did. With my record, I am the last man who could sell you a benefit.

That is enough about me.

## Where the method comes from {-}

I didn't invent this way of quitting. A man named Allen Carr did, for smokers. His book, *The Easy Way to Stop Smoking*, has helped a great many people put down cigarettes without a fight. The free book that helped me took Carr's method and turned it on porn. This book borrows from both, and takes the same method to lust itself. The credit is theirs. Any mistakes are mine.

## If you believe, and if you don't {-}

The best pictures of this trap are in the Bible, so this book quotes it. If you don't believe, read it as you would an old book that turns out to know you. When you hear God or sin mentioned, take it as your conscience talking. If you hold another faith, bring it with you.

And if you are a Christian, hear one verse early. Romans 8:1 says, "There is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus". When this book says "sin", it is telling the truth. It is not beating you.

Paul, the apostle who wrote much of the New Testament, told the Christians in Rome to "be transformed by the renewing of your mind". He didn't say to try harder. "Be transformed" is something done to you, as your mind is made new. A believer will say God does it. If you're not one, call it changing your mind. Either way, it starts with seeing clearly.

So start with the Little Monster. If he does nothing for you, why has every attempt to be rid of him failed? The best answer anyone has given is two thousand years old. It is a story about a house.
