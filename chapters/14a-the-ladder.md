# The Ladder {#the-ladder}

Here is every man in the trap, from the shallow end to the deep. Find yourself in the list. You may be in it more than once. And notice, as you go, that every one of them has a reason why he is not as bad as the next man.

## The shallow end {-}

- **The man who never started.** He's rare, and he shouldn't be smug about it. He's outside the trap by upbringing, luck or the grace of God. Every man inside it was once sure he'd never be caught.
- **The beginner.** He's usually a boy of twelve or thirteen. Don't envy him. He's a fly strolling about on the rim of the jar. Every nightly man started out as an occasional one.
- **The nightly man.** Once a day, in bed, lights off. He calls it his sleeping pill. He eases his itch for ten minutes in every twenty-four hours and carries it for the rest. He walks all day with a stone in his shoe so that he can enjoy taking it out at bedtime.
- **The streak counter.** He keeps a count of his clean days. When he's doing it, he wishes he weren't. When he's stopped, he feels deprived and counts the days. He has the worst of both. His cousin, the retention monk, is stopping in order to get something: a deeper voice, a glow, women's heads turning. He has only swapped one kind of counting for another.

## The screen {-}

- **The man on porn.** This is the heavy end, and most men in the trap have spent time here. The screen never runs out, so he loses hours where another man loses minutes. He ends up watching things that would have sickened him at eighteen. He isn't a monster. The way out is the same for him, and it is no harder.
- **The man who quit porn and kept the hand.** He tells himself it's a different thing now. He has only cut down. He's still feeding the same Little Monster, and on some bad night the monster will remember where the stronger food is kept.
- **The man who "only reads".** He doesn't watch videos. He reads sex stories, or he looks at drawings and cartoons, which the internet calls hentai, a Japanese word. "No real woman is involved", he says. But the lust is real, and it is his. Jesus put the sin in the heart, and the heart is the same whether the page holds a photograph or a drawing.
- **The man with a machine.** This one arrived in the 2020s. He talks every night to a girlfriend made by a computer. She is always awake and always pleased with him. She never has a bad day and never says no. Or he has the computer make pictures of women to his order. In one American survey in 2025, nearly a third of young men said they had chatted with a machine as a boyfriend or girlfriend. Of everyone in the survey who had tried it, about one in five said they liked it better than talking to a real person. C.S. Lewis said the habit gives a man a harem of imaginary brides. This is that harem with a voice added. Now the walls talk back.

## Real women {-}

- **The man led by women.** His lust goes out through real women. He follows, flatters, pays and waits. Or he lives on the dating apps, swiping every night and meeting nobody. Or he's married and keeps a "harmless" flirtation going at work. He thinks nothing has happened. Ask his wife whether nothing has happened.
- **The collector.** He goes from bed to bed and keeps count, and he takes the number as proof that he is somebody.
- **The man who pays.** He would never go to a prostitute. But he pays a monthly fee to see one woman's private pictures, and he tips her so that she will write back. Or he sends money to a woman who plays video games on a live stream, so that she will say his name. Or it's the strip club. It is a big business. In 2024, men spent more than seven billion dollars on the largest of these subscription sites. And this is what that money often buys. Reporters found that many of the women there never write the messages at all. Agencies hire workers to do it, called chatters. Many of them are men, sitting in offices on the other side of the world, paid to type like her and to keep him spending. He thinks he has a girlfriend. He is a customer, and even when she is kind to him, it is her job. If this is you, add up what you have paid this year and look at the number. Then ask what it bought you.
- **The man who buys it in person.** An escort, a massage parlor with a back room, or a trip abroad to a place where sex is cheap and nobody knows him. He tells himself it is at least honest.
- **The single man who'll stop when he's married.** Ask any married man how that went. A wedding doesn't end the itch. "When I'm married" is the most comfortable way there is of saying "not yet". If you intend to give a woman your whole self one day, the kindest thing you can do for her is to arrive already free.

That is every man in the trap. Three of them need more than a line, because each has an "at least" that sounds like a good one. They are the married man, the man who keeps it all in his head, and the church man who has never told anyone.
