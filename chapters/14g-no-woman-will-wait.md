# No Woman Will Wait {#no-woman-will-wait}

The first man has no fiancée. He is dating, or he would like to be. And he has done the arithmetic.

"No woman my age will date a man who won't sleep with her. If I hold that line, I'll be alone."

Start with who leaves. Some women will. Tell a woman on the third date that you are keeping the bed for marriage. She may be gone by the fourth. Look at what you have learned, and how cheaply you learned it. She may have wanted the nights, or she may find the rule strange. That is no insult to her. Either way, she doesn't want what you want, and you needed to know that.

Then look at who stays. She has heard a man say that he wants her and that he can wait for her. Very few men have said both to her. It doesn't prove that he will stay. It shows her that his wanting doesn't run him.

Now look at the way you are afraid to lose. What has it picked for you, year after year? It picked women who would sleep with a man who had promised nothing. And it picked you for them, a man who would take that and go. You know how those ended. You have the record.

The old way doesn't find a wife faster. It fills the years with nights, so a man never notices he isn't looking.

## How to say it {-}

You may dread the sentence itself. Say it early and plainly, and don't preach. "I like you. I've decided to keep sex for marriage. That is about me. It is no verdict on you."

If she laughs, let her laugh. If she asks why, tell her the truth. You have seen what the other way did to you. If she asks about the women before her, tell her the truth about that too.

And don't hide behind the line. A man who has stopped using women still has to cross the room, ask her to dinner, and risk a no. The line doesn't make that easier. It makes you a man who can hear the no and stay himself.

## A smaller field {-}

Does the line shrink the field? It does. It shrinks it to the women who want what you want. That was always the only field you had.

Be honest about the size of that field. It may be small. Many of the women in it will be believers, and they will ask where you stand. Tell them the truth. Some will say no to that, and some won't. Either way, you both know early. And the field is wider than the women who already hold this line. It includes any woman who hears your reasons and says yes. Some have had enough of the other road themselves. But compare the two fields fairly. The wide one has had you for decades, and you have searched it. It gave you nights and no wife. And waiting is not stalling. A man who has watched a woman with clear eyes doesn't need five years to know his mind.

That leaves the second man in the hall. He isn't dating, and he has stopped expecting to.
