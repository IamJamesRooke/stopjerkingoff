# The Back Door {#the-back-door}

> "All things are lawful for me," but I will not be brought under the power of anything.
>
> — 1 Corinthians 6:12

"Fine. No porn. But the hand on its own is natural."

That is the knock from the second room, and nearly every man has stood in it. This is the hand and the orgasm with no screen. The forums write it MO, and men think of it as the mild dose. Some men have read every page so far and quietly filed it under porn. The screen was the problem, they decide, and the hand without the screen is another matter. A man quits porn and is proud of it. For the women on the screen it is a real change. Nobody is being used for him now. For him, nothing has changed. He keeps the hand, the way a man gives up whiskey and keeps the beer.

He says it like this. "I'm jerking off, but at least I'm not watching porn."

Listen to the sentence. Nobody says "at least" about a thing he is glad of. He says it about a thing he is defending.

Then ask what changed upstairs. Nothing did. The Little Monster got his dinner. He can't tell a banquet from a sandwich, and he bangs just as loud tomorrow. Men call it mild. The hold is the same. A drunk on beer is still a drunk.

The hand needs no screen, no woman and no money. All it needs is a man by himself for five minutes. For most men it was the first thing lust ever borrowed, and they expect it to be the last to go.

Its lie is different from the lie of the screen. The screen says, "This is exciting." The hand says, "This is nothing." It's natural. It's free. Nobody gets hurt. It's just maintenance, like trimming your nails.

Some men in the church at Corinth, a Greek city, had a slogan like that: "All things are lawful for me." Paul let them have it, and then answered it: "but I will not be brought under the power of anything."

One man quit porn twice, and went back twice. Both times, he said, masturbation was what led him back.

It isn't hard to see how. The hand never works alone. Ask what is playing while it happens, because something always is. It's a memory, or a face from the feed, or a woman from work. The hand is where the look and the thought turn into the act. For the man who quit porn, the film is usually porn.

Men have a name for the store of pictures a man keeps in his head for this. They call it the spank bank. A man who draws on it is still watching porn. He has moved the screen inside his head. It is weaker there, the way fruit juice is weaker than a can of cola. It is the same sugar, and it does the same work.

But a memory wears thin. It does a little less each time. So the hand goes looking for a stronger film, and it knows exactly where that film is kept. The man bolted the front door and left the back door standing open. Then he was surprised to find the same old visitor in his kitchen.

Suppose your hand never leads you back to the screen. The trade is the same without it. You ease an itch for ten minutes, and the easing sets it going again.

So what has the hand really been paying you all these years?

The finish feels good. Nobody denies it. But look at what the good feeling is made of. Most of it is the itch stopping, and the last time set the itch going. The rest is a few sweet seconds, like sugar, paid for before and after. For ten minutes a man feels the way the man who never does it feels all day. That is all it has ever paid.

Then there is what the hand teaches. A body learns that desire is something to be dealt with quickly, alone, and with nobody to answer to. A man practices that for fifteen years. Then he marries, and wonders why the lesson came with him.

And if he never marries, he has still spent his best hours alone behind a locked door.

"But it's only me now. Nobody else is hurt." Then look at the one man who is. He is the man this whole book is about. His evenings go to it. His word goes, each time he says "never again". The push that would have sent him across a room to speak to a woman goes down the drain instead. "Only me" is not a small thing to lose.

The world calls this self-love. It is a strange kind of love that needs the door locked and leaves you feeling worse than before.

Here is a test to carry through the rooms that are left. Ask one question of anything you do. Is this a gift, with a person at the other end of it? Or is it a hit I'm taking? A gift is given to someone, and you stay. A hit is taken, and you leave. The hand gives nothing to anyone.

So the hand is not the harmless one. It holds the door open for all the rest.

The terms at this door are the same as at the first. No vow and no count. Has the hand ever paid you anything but relief from its own itch? If it hasn't, there is nothing in this room either, and the door is shut.

That leaves the wanting itself. It was behind the screen and the hand all along. Shut both doors and it doesn't lie down. It goes looking, and it brings the hand back if it can.

The uninvited guest picks up his blanket and moves down the hall. "I'm looking, but at least it isn't porn."
