# The Back Door {#the-back-door}

> "All things are lawful for me," but I will not be brought under the power of anything.
>
> — 1 Corinthians 6:12

"Fine. No porn. But the hand on its own is natural."

That's the knock from the second room, and nearly every man has stood in it. The hand and the orgasm, no screen. The forums write it MO, and men think of it as the mild dose. Plenty of them read every page so far and quietly filed it under porn. The screen was the problem, they figure, not the hand. So a man quits porn and he's proud of it. For the women on the screen, that's a real change. Nobody's being used for him now. For him, nothing has changed. He keeps the hand, the way a man gives up whiskey and keeps the beer.

He says it like this. "I'm jerking off, but at least I'm not watching porn."

Listen to that sentence. Nobody says "at least" about a thing he's glad of. He says it about a thing he's defending.

What changed upstairs? Nothing. The Little Monster got his dinner. He can't tell a banquet from a sandwich, and he'll bang just as loud tomorrow. Men call it mild. A drunk on beer is still a drunk.

The hand needs no screen, no woman and no money. Just a man alone for five minutes. It was the first thing lust ever borrowed from most men, and they figure it'll be the last to go.

Its lie is different from the screen's. The screen says, "This is exciting." The hand says, "This is nothing." Natural. Free. Nobody gets hurt. Maintenance, like trimming your nails.

Some men in the church at Corinth, a Greek city, had a slogan like that: "All things are lawful for me." Paul let them have it, and then answered it: "but I will not be brought under the power of anything."

One man quit porn twice, and went back twice. Both times, he said, masturbation was what led him back.

Not hard to see how. The hand never works alone. Something's always playing while it happens. A memory, a face from the feed, a woman from the elevator. The hand is where the look and the thought turn into the act. For the man who quit porn, the film is usually porn.

Men have a name for the pictures stored in the head for this. The spank bank. A man who draws on it is still watching porn. He's just moved the screen inside his skull. It's the same thing in a weaker dose. Think of a cigarette a man rolls himself, beside one from a factory. The factory one was built by people paid to hook him faster. Both are tobacco, and his lungs can't tell which is which.

![](images/illustrations/back-door-juice-and-cola.png){.illustration width=80%}

And a memory wears thin. So the hand goes shopping for a stronger film, and it knows exactly where that film is kept. The man bolted the front door and left the back door wide open. Now he's shocked to find the same old visitor in his kitchen.

Even if your hand never leads you back to the screen, the trade is the same. You ease an itch for ten minutes, and the easing sets it going again.

So what has the hand really been paying you all these years?

The finish feels good. Nobody denies it. But look at what the good feeling is made of. Mostly it's the itch stopping, and the last time started that itch. The rest is a few sweet seconds, like sugar, paid for before and after. For ten minutes a man feels the way the man who never does it feels all day. That's the whole paycheck.

Then there's what the hand teaches. A body learns that desire is something to dump fast, alone, with nobody to answer to. A man practices that for fifteen years. Then he marries, and the lesson comes along on the honeymoon.

And if he never marries, he's still spent his best hours alone behind a locked door.

"But it's only me now. Nobody else is hurt." Then look at the one man who is. He's the man this whole book is about. His evenings go to it. His word goes, every time he says "never again". The push that would've sent him across a room to talk to a woman goes down the drain. "Only me" is no small thing to lose.

The world calls this self-love. Some love. It needs the door locked and leaves you feeling worse.

Here's a test to carry through the rooms that are left. Ask of anything you do, is this a gift, with a person at the other end? Or is it a hit I'm taking? A gift is given to someone, and you stay. A hit is taken, and you leave. The hand gives nothing to anyone.

The hand isn't the harmless one. It holds the door open for all the rest.

The terms at this door are the same as at the first. No vow, no count. Has the hand ever paid you anything but relief from its own itch? If not, there's nothing in this room either, and the door is shut.

That leaves the wanting itself. It was behind the screen and the hand all along. Shut both doors and it doesn't lie down. It goes looking, and it drags the hand back if it can.

The uninvited guest picks up his blanket and moves down the hall. "I'm looking, but at least it isn't porn."
