# About the Author {- #about-the-author}

Then he noticed he wasn't the only one. The boat's full of men, and it's a very large boat. The world has plenty of problems, and it needs its men awake to face them. A man run by this habit runs at maybe forty percent. No study produced that number. Having been that man did. Until the habit is broken, he never gets to what he was made for.

That's why the book went out to everyone. His name isn't on it, because he isn't the point. You are.
