# Mothers, Sisters, Daughters {#mothers-sisters-daughters}

The feed and the reel are made of women who aren't there. The same eye works on the ones who are: the second look in the next aisle, the conversation at work that runs a little longer than it needs to.

Paul gave a young pastor a rule for this that is hard to improve on. Treat "the elder women as mothers; the younger as sisters, in all purity."

Carry it one step further, and it covers every woman you will ever meet. The older ones are mothers. The ones your own age are sisters. The young ones are daughters.

A man doesn't size up his sister, or perform for her, or keep a door open with her. He doesn't flatter his mother to get something. He would walk through fire for his daughter, and he wants nothing from her at all. He's simply glad they are there.

Imagine living like that for one week, in every shop, office and street. Think how much quieter your head would be.

Women can tell the difference at once. Most of them have been looked over by men since they were girls, and they know that look from across a room. A man who talks to them the way he talks to anyone stands out, because he is rare. Don't do it for that. A man who does it to be noticed is back to performing. Just know that it is so.

It changes the woman you're dating as well. Nothing is wrong with wanting a beautiful wife. But beauty can blind a man, and a blind man signs anything. So take her face out of the question for a minute, and ask two plain things. Do I really like being with her? Can I build a life with her? A man ruled by lust can't answer those. He has never been calm enough near her to find out.

A free man can.

## The app {-}

The feed has a cousin, and it looks respectable. It's the dating app.

There is nothing wrong with wanting a wife, or with meeting her through a phone. Plenty of good marriages began that way. But be honest about why you open it. A man looking for a wife reads slowly and writes to one woman. A hungry man swipes. He goes through fifty faces in five minutes and sorts each one in half a second. What he's after is the small jolt when one of them matches.

One man told a newspaper that he swiped yes on every woman the app showed him. At his worst he was talking to ten at once. He wasn't looking for any of them. He wanted to know that women found him attractive.

Another man made it plainer still. He had been on one app for eight years. Then he met a woman at a party, and they decided to get married. He wrote on a forum that he was still swiping, though he never spoke to a single match. He had what the app was supposed to be for, and he couldn't put it down. Somebody answered him, "Don't you get it? You won!"

That is the same itch in better clothes. The swipe is the scroll, with a chance attached.

So the test is simple. Are you looking for a wife, or looking for a hit? If it's a wife, you won't need fifty at a time. If it's a hit, you know what to call it.

## The one girl {-}

The app is fifty faces. The opposite case is one.

You check your phone forty times an hour. You've read her last message until you know it by heart, and you write your answer five times before you send it. You know when she was last online. It feels like love. It even feels like the opposite of the harem, because she is one woman and she is real.

Look closer. You aren't thinking about her. You're thinking about what you want from her, and about how you look to her. What you care about is the feeling you have for her, and the longing. She has become a picture in your head, and you're texting the picture. It's the harem with one face in it.

A free man can like a woman, tell her so plainly, and get on with his day while he waits for her answer. If she says no, he's sorry, and he is still himself. That's the kind of man a woman can say yes to.

## The pictures {-}

One more thing passes between a man and the girl on his mind, and that is pictures. You ask her for one, or you send one of yourself and hope. Be honest about what you're doing. You are making your own porn, with a woman you know. You've turned her into something to look at before you have learned who she is. And a picture, once sent, belongs to whoever holds the phone. A free man doesn't ask for it. If she offers, he says no, and she will remember that he did.

## Seeing people again {-}

A man on the NoFap forum, a hundred days out, noticed that the women around him had changed. Of course it was his eyes that had changed. He had stopped scanning their bodies, he wrote, and "started noticing much more interesting things about them. Smiles, gestures". He was seeing people again.

Set him beside the man who quit porn and kept the feed. That man is still standing at his red line, with a phone full of crumbs and a head full of women who aren't there.

AND HE CALLS IT BEING FREE!

That is the eye and the mind. Next comes the one most men know best.
