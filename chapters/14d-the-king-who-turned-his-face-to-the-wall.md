# The King Who Turned His Face to the Wall {#the-king-who-turned-his-face-to-the-wall}

Samson was led by a woman he wanted. The next man is a quieter case, and more men are like him. He was a king named Ahab, and his wife was Jezebel. This chapter is for the married man, and for any man a woman can lead by what he wants.

Ahab wanted a vineyard that lay next to his palace. The owner, a man named Naboth, wouldn't sell. The land had been in his family for generations. So the king went home. He "laid himself down on his bed, and turned away his face, and would eat no bread." He was a grown man and a king, and he sulked like a child because someone had told him no.

Jezebel found him there. "Do you now govern the kingdom of Israel?" she said. "Arise, and eat bread, and let your heart be merry. I will give you the vineyard". Then she "wrote letters in Ahab's name, and sealed them with his seal". The letters arranged for Naboth to be accused of a crime he had not committed and stoned to death.

Ahab asked no questions. He didn't ask what she meant to do, or how the vineyard had suddenly come free. When she told him Naboth was dead, he got up and went down to take it.

Jezebel was a murderer, and the Bible doesn't spare her. But it was his name on the letters and his seal in the wax. He didn't lend them because he was afraid of her. He lent them because she was getting him what he wanted, and a man who is being given what he wants doesn't ask how. The Bible's verdict on him is that he "sold himself".

Mark Driscoll, an American pastor, gives the men of his church a short rule drawn from that marriage: "If you tolerate, they will dominate." Ahab tolerated everything, because his silence had been paid for.

There is a simple way to find out whether you are Ahab. Say no to something, kindly and plainly, and see what it costs you. If you already know what it will cost, and that is why you haven't said it in years, then someone has a hand on the lever.

## The free man in the room {-}

So what does a man look like with the lever gone? Put him in a room full of women, at any party.

He can see that a woman is beautiful. It simply stops deciding anything. He doesn't buy the drink or laugh at the joke that wasn't funny. He doesn't give her the favor, the job or the last word because of her face. He treats her as he treats everyone else, which is fairly.

That is better for him. He keeps his money, his judgment and his self-respect. It is better for her too, because she gets dealt with as a person. And it is better for the plain woman standing beside her, whom thirsty men have looked straight through all her life.

## The lever at home {-}

It matters far more at home, with his wife. Ahab's silence was bought with a vineyard. In most houses it is bought with something else.

There's a man who gives in on everything. When his wife is unfair to him, or plainly out of line, he says nothing, because he is afraid of what it will cost him that night. Sex has become a lever in that house. Some women pull that lever on purpose, and that is wrong of them. Others never give it a thought. But he can't change her. What he can change is whether the lever works.

Now take the man whose lust no longer runs him. He can say, quite calmly, "That isn't right, and I won't go along with it." If she tells him he can sleep on the couch tonight, he picks up a pillow and goes. To him it feels like camping.

He isn't being cold, and he isn't punishing her. He still loves her, and he still wants her. But he can't be bought with it, and he can't be starved with it, so he is free to tell her the truth. That is better for her as well. She can trust his yes, because he is a man who is able to say no.

Samson was the strongest man alive, and Ahab was a king, and neither of them could say no. The man with the pillow under his arm is neither strong nor a king. He is only free, and that turns out to be the one thing that matters. Nobody can lead him by what he wants, because it no longer leads him.

That is the chase. Samson and Ahab were led. The next man is sure that he is the one doing the leading.
