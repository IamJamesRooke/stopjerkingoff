# The Final Step

> "Stand firm therefore in the liberty by which Christ has made us free, and don't be entangled again with a yoke of bondage."
>
> — Galatians 5:1

You know what lust is, and you know what you are for. You are ready for the final step.

It is not a final session. There is no "one last time" in this book. Nobody sins once more for old times' sake on his way out of a sin. And you don't need to. Whenever your last time was, it's behind you now. You didn't mark it with a ceremony, and that's the right send-off for a thing that was never worth anything.

If you had already stopped before you got here, good. Stay stopped, and take this step all the same. It says out loud what you have been doing.

The final step is a decision, spoken out loud. For a believer it is a prayer, and for a man who doesn't pray there is a version right after it. Before you take it, ask yourself one thing. Does some part of you still believe lust has something to give you? If so, don't take the step yet.

One thing doesn't count against you. The orgasm feels good for a few seconds, and nobody here says otherwise. Sugar tastes sweet to the man it is ruining. The question is whether those seconds are a benefit. Name the one thing you still think it gives you. Go back to that one room and look again. Then come straight back here. Reading the whole book again is one more way of saying later.

You don't have to feel brave, and you don't need a clean record behind you. A man who has failed fifty times takes this step exactly like a man who has never tried.

Nobody *decides* to fall into this trap. You wandered in as a boy. But the trap is built to hold you for life, and nobody wanders out of it. Getting out takes one clear, positive decision, and you're about to make it.

## "But I've promised this before" {-}

Of course you have, and you broke it every time. You may be afraid of breaking another.

What were you promising then? You promised to go without something you still wanted. That was a vow against yourself, and half of you never signed it. Today is different, because there's nothing left that you want from lust. You're only putting into words what has already happened in your head.

So don't think about failing. This time you aren't climbing by willpower. You're walking out through an open door, and you can enjoy the walk. If you're a Christian, you also have something stronger than your own word to stand on. A Christian counselor wrote it about pornography, and it holds for every form lust takes: "Jesus' grace to change you is stronger than pornography's power to destroy you."

Maybe you feel you've failed too often to come back again. Hear a man who felt exactly that. He pictures the prodigal son, the runaway in Jesus's story, halfway home. In his picture the son protests, "But God, I still smell like yesterday." And the Father answers with two words: "Just come." He adds, for every man reading: "You're not too dirty. You're not too far."

Jesus asked for plain speech: "But let your 'Yes' be 'Yes' and your 'No' be 'No.'" That is all this step is: a plain No that you mean. You are showing the Little Monster the door, once, and you already know that he has nothing you want. He may knock for a few days. Nobody is going to answer.

## If there is another person {-}

One thing comes before the prayer. Make sure that nothing you are doing breaks the law, and that nothing you are doing is harming another person. If something is, you already know what it is. Don't carry it past today.

The No you are about to say covers any bed outside marriage. Don't take that as a rule somebody handed you. Check it one last time against your own years. Name what any bed outside a promise left you that stayed. Then ask the old question. Suppose you had kept sex for the woman you promised to keep. Would your decisions have been better, or worse?

If a woman you love is in that bed, this is not a No to her. It is a No to the bed until the promise is made. The best thing you can bring her is a man who doesn't need to take.

## The prayer {-}

Here is how.

**1. Go somewhere you can be alone and speak out loud.** Kneel if you're able. A man's body teaches his mind what's going on.

**2. Pray this.** Use your own words if you prefer, but don't make it long or clever. Near the end you will ask to be shown a man to go back for. He is some man you know who is still where you were. A freed man goes back for one.

> Father, I confess it.
>
> I have used my body, my eyes and my mind for lust. I have looked at women as things to use. I have done it in secret, told myself it was nothing, and hidden it from the people who love me. It was sin, and I am sorry.
>
> Forgive me, as you promised you would. Make me clean.
>
> It never gave me anything. I am not giving anything up. I am being set free.
>
> Today I am finished with it. I am done with masturbation. I am done with porn. I am done with using a woman, and with any bed outside marriage. I will not feed lust with my eyes, my imagination or the chase. My answer is No, and I give it gladly.
>
> I can't keep this by my own strength, and I'm not going to try. Keep me. Make me a man who can be trusted, and make me fit for whatever you made me for. Then show me the man I am to go back for.
>
> Thank you that I am forgiven. Thank you that I am free.
>
> In Jesus's name. Amen.

**3. If you don't pray, say this instead.** Say it out loud, on your feet, to the man you intend to be. If you hold another faith, say it to God in the words your own faith gives you.

> I admit it.
>
> Lust has been making my decisions. It has cost me time, sleep, money and self-respect. I kept it secret, told myself it was nothing, and hid it from the people who love me. Where I have used anyone, on a screen or off it, that was wrong, and I am sorry.
>
> Today I am finished with it. I am done with masturbation. I am done with porn. I am done with using anyone, and with any bed outside marriage. I will not feed it with my eyes, my imagination or the chase. My answer is No, and I give it gladly.
>
> I won't do this by gritting my teeth. I'll do it by remembering what I now know: a few good seconds, and no benefit at all.
>
> I will be a man who can be trusted, and fit for the people and the work that are mine. And I will go back for one man who is still where I was.
>
> I am free.

Add what it cost you, in your own words. Don't cut anything from the No.

That is the decision, and it counts in full. A decision you've only thought is easy to un-think. One you've spoken has been heard, if only by you. If one day you want to pray the other version, it will still be here.

If you're Catholic, take this to Confession as well, and soon. The Church asks Catholics to bring sins like this to a priest. Many men say that was the moment the weight came off. Your priest has heard it all before, and he is bound never to repeat a word of it.

**4. Then stand up and say it:**

**"YES! I'M FREE!"**

Shout it if the neighbors can stand it.

Don't get up thinking "I must never do that again" or "I'm not allowed anymore". Those are a prisoner's sentences. Get up thinking, "Isn't it wonderful? I never have to do that again. I'm free."

Do it now.
