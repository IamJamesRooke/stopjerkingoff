# Outline: Stop Jerking Off: The Easy Peasy Guide to Quitting Masturbation and Conquering Lust

Anonymous. This follows EasyPeasy chapter by chapter: same order, same job for each chapter, same voice, with fresh text adapted to jerking off. Each entry lists the chapter's job, the key moves, a verse (World English Bible), and what to research before drafting.

**Global rules**
- Voice: EasyPeasy's. Second person, confident, repetitive, slogans in capitals, short paragraphs, the occasional joke.
- Faith: the Christian scaffold sits underneath. Roughly one verse per chapter, used like an epigraph or a punchline, never a sermon. For non-believers: "If you're an atheist, play along. Call it mindful self-talk if that helps."
- Audience: men. A short note here and there on how this applies to women.
- Self-control thread: "A man who can't control his lust can't be trusted." Mastering lust is the root; quitting the habit is the visible fruit.
- Claims about drive and harm are conviction plus testimony. No invented studies. Semen retention is "try it and see".
- Quotes: real, verbatim, anonymised ("a reader on r/NoFapChristians").
- Glossary: **MO** (masturbation and orgasm, as in the original); **the Little Monster** (the urge); **the Big Monster** (the lies you believe about it).
